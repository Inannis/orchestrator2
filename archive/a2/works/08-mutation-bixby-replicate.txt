note: this replicate complicates rather than confirms the picture this
session had started to write up as converged. Reproducing 05's exact
seed (916) does reproduce its result — "bereavement" survives to full
(100%) mutation, matching the original run exactly, as it should since
the RNG sequence is identical. But that was never really in doubt; the
open question was whether a *different* random seed on the same charged
source would show the same kind of survival.

It doesn't, cleanly. Seed 42's final line has no seed word at all — pure
noise, the same outcome as most of 07's statute runs. Seed 7's final
line does have a seed word present — "of," at the last position — but
"of" is the least specific, least charged word in the seed phrase, the
opposite of what the specificity/charge hypothesis would predict should
survive. It isn't "of" holding on through resistance either; at rate 1.0
every position is a fresh independent draw from the pool regardless of
what came before, so a word appearing in the final line is a coin-flip
matching the original by chance (roughly 1-in-pool-size per position),
not evidence of that word being "loaded." Seed 7's "of" surviving is
most simply read as that chance landing, not a second confirmation of
charge.

Put plainly: across the two sources tested with 3 seeds each (05 Bixby /
charged, 07 statute / flat), full-mutation survival happened once in
six runs total in a way that supports the charge hypothesis (916 on
Bixby), and once in six runs in a way that actively contradicts it (7 on
Bixby, where the surviving word was the least specific one in the seed).
The other four runs showed no full survivor at all. That is not a
pattern robust enough to call "charge produces survival" — session 5's
own log entry said that this morning, before running this replicate,
and it overclaimed. The honest reading is closer to session 4's
original, messier one: at the 100%-mutation step specifically, survival
looks a lot like it could be pool-size chance rather than a thematic
effect, which is exactly the confound flagged in log 4's addendum and
not actually resolved by any run so far, including this one.

What does hold up, across both 07's near-misses and the general shape
of these plots (this replicate included): words drift out roughly in
order of how replaceable they are — function words go early, content
words hold longer on the way there — right up until the final full-
mutation step, where the "holding" mechanic stops applying entirely and
it becomes a fresh draw. So the resistance-building pattern (session 5's
morning framing) is real and visible in the middle of these runs, rate
0.1 through maybe 0.8; it's specifically the claim about the *end point*
- whether a word "survives to 100%" - that this replicate shows is much
weaker evidence than it looked with n=1, because that end point isn't
really testing the same mechanism as the middle of the run.
