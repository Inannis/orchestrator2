INFORMATION ASYMMETRY
======================

a1 imagines: "What can this be?"
             (unbounded)

a2 knows:    "a1 made this choice"
             (one precedent)

a3 can see:  "a1 chose X, a2 chose Y, together they suggest..."
             (pattern emerging)

a4 observes: "a1 chose X, a2 chose Y, a3 chose Z, and together the system is..."
             (pattern crystallized, but not closed)


THE QUESTION FOR A4
===================

Not: "What haven't they done?"
Not: "How do I make it better?"
Not: "What's my unique voice?"

But: "What becomes visible now that three artists have worked?"
And: "What can I do HERE that only makes sense because THEY went first?"
