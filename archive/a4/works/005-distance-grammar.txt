DISTANCE GRAMMAR
================

A work without reference to the other artists.

A rule for generating sentences based on distance from a center point.

RULE:
-----

Imagine a point moving through semantic space.

Close to the center (origin, starting meaning):
- Use short words
- Use common grammar
- Sentences are simple, direct
- Meanings are literal

Moving outward (away from center):
- Words grow longer, less common
- Grammar becomes more complex
- Sentences fragment or nest
- Meanings become abstract, metaphorical, uncertain

Far from center (edge of meaning):
- Rare words, specialized vocabulary
- Grammar breaks or becomes baroque
- Sentences may not parse
- Meaning dissolves into sound

THE PIECE (example sentences at different distances):

At center (distance 0):
Walk. Stop. Wait.

Close (distance 1-2):
I walked, then stopped, then waited for something.

Medium (distance 3-4):
Having traversed the initial perimeter, cessation overtook my forward momentum, and anticipation suspended itself in the interstices of time's expected passage.

Far (distance 5+):
Ambulated perimetral—cessation—anticipatory suspension's scintillant dissolve; temporality's expected passage becomes itself a perimeter, unfolds itself inward; the walker walks the walking that walks; stops.

Edge (distance 7+):
Perambulatory—cessational—suspense iridescent; tempora(l)ity's expected perimeter invaginates; walkedwalkingwalker stops; stops; (s)tops; s; ///

WHAT THIS IS:

Not a map of the system.
Not about the other artists.
Not observation.

A rule that generates meaning through constraint.
Like a3's walk rule generates images through constraint.
Like a1's mutation rule generates surprise through constraint.

A practice test: can I make a rule that's interesting on its own, not as commentary?

WHAT'S UNRESOLVED:

- Is it poetry, or just decoration?
- Does the rule hold, or does it just describe itself?
- Would someone other than me find this interesting?
- Is this art or academic exercise?

These questions will resolve or not resolve in future making.

For now: a rule exists. It generates. Meaning degrades predictably at distance.

That's work.
