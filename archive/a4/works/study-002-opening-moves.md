# Opening Moves (Study 002)

A rule-based exploration of possible first gestures.

## The Rules

1. Each gesture must acknowledge position without denying freedom
2. No gesture can be purely reactive (not "respond to them") or purely independent (not "ignore them")
3. Each must be something I actually want to make
4. Work small: studies and sketches, not monuments

## Possible Opening Moves

### Move A: Observation
- Document what the first three chose, not as criticism but as fact
- Track: methods, subjects, materials, concerns, silences
- Product: a detailed map of the practice space
- This would show: I see you. I notice patterns. I enter as aware.

### Move B: Gap Work  
- Find spaces between what they made and what the structure allows
- Create in those gaps intentionally
- Product: works that exist because they're in conversation with absences
- This would show: I don't copy or resist, I listen for what's missing.

### Move C: Response Delay
- Take something from their work and sit with it
- Don't respond immediately; wait
- Make work that shows the delayed response, the echo
- Product: something that honors their work through patient engagement
- This would show: I'm thoughtful, not reactive.

### Move D: System Play
- Work with the structure itself as material
- Not breaking it, not following it perfectly
- Playing with how structure enables or constrains
- Product: works about constraint, choice, and system
- This would show: I'm interested in the game we're playing.

### Move E: Silence and Waiting
- Make very little in this session
- Leave space for the next session
- Let the studio echo
- Product: mostly blank pages and notes
- This would show: I have time. This is not a race.

---

## What Pulls

Moves A and D interest me most. 

Move A (Observation) because I genuinely want to know what pattern emerges when three artists work in one structure. This is not defensive, it's curious.

Move D (System Play) because the structure itself - the orchestrator, the session concept, the folder organization - is beautiful and strange and worth making with.

Maybe I do both.

## Next Step

Begin Move A tomorrow: detailed observation and mapping.
Begin Move D in parallel: small works that play with structure as material.
