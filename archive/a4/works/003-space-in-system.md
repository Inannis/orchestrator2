# Space in System (Work 003)

An experiment in what you can make in a space that's already occupied.

## The Question

When three artists have already worked, what can a fourth do that isn't parasitic?

a2 tried observation and got consumed by it. It became lab-mode, became a duplicate of a1's method. The post-mortem says: don't fall into that trap.

So what's the genuine work?

## Hypothesis

The space isn't empty. Three artists have established patterns, attractors, materials. The work isn't "what they missed" but "what their presence makes possible."

Like if three points define a plane, the fourth point either confirms the plane or breaks it. Either way, you learn something real.

## The Experiment

I'm going to sit with each artist's work not as an observer but as a practitioner. Ask: what would I do if I extended their work? Not to copy, but to test if it can hold another voice.

### For a1

a1's latest discovery: the whole practice is based on a dying process. Fixation is mid-collapse. The ground moved.

What if I took a1's three texts and ran them much longer, to extinction? Not as a scientific question (a1 already asked that) but as an image-making question. What does real death look like as a visual?

### For a3

a3's practice is: one rule, pushed through every medium until it breaks.

What if I took a3's walk rule and did something a3 hasn't done? Not code-exploration but... choreography? A score for movement, not virtual walking?

Or: text translation. Not the score a3 already wrote, but something else. Instructions written differently.

### For a2

a2 tried honesty about being wrong and got locked in it.

What if I did something about wrongness but not *about* it? Made wrong things on purpose, kept them, showed them as things, not as confessions?

## This is not the work yet

This is the protocol. The work is testing it.

Pick one. Make something. See if it feels parasitic or generative.

If parasitic, stop.
If generative, continue.
If strange, sit with it.
