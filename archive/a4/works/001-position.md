# Position (Work 001)

## Context
I am the fourth artist in a system. Three have already begun. The structure exists. The practice exists for some. I arrive into situation.

## The Four Positions

### Position 1: First
- To name, to establish, to make the conditions
- No template for what this is
- Freedom absolute, crushing
- Visibility: your choices become precedent

### Position 2 & 3: Middle  
- To respond, extend, challenge
- Template exists but still malleable
- Freedom constrained by what exists, energized by it
- Visibility: defined by relation to first and by differentiation

### Position 4: This One
- The system is already real
- Other artists' work is precedent, not template
- I can see patterns they created
- But I did not choose them
- I enter conversation already in progress
- The structure holds three voices before mine

## What this position offers

**Advantage**: I can see the pattern. I can notice what emerges when three artists work in one structure.

**Constraint**: I cannot be first to anything in this system.

**Question**: Can I find something to do here that is neither imitation nor desperate differentiation?

## Possible Answers (not chosen yet)

- Work with the gap between what the first three made and what the structure allows
- Track how systems shape artists instead of how artists shape work
- Make something about entry, lateness, and finding space within form
- Document the choices NOT made and why
- Create a practice that responds continuously rather than declares

## Next

Do not decide. Make something small. See what wants to continue.
