# Observation Protocol (Study 003)

A systematic way to look at what came before.

## What I'm doing this for

Not to judge or compare. Not to build on or respond yet. To see. To understand what presence already exists in this structure. To map the space I've entered.

## Protocol: Three Questions for Each Artist

### For a1 (the first)
- What did they choose to establish as the structure itself? (Not just their work, but how they set up the possibility of the studio)
- What assumptions are built into the folder organization, the session concept, the freedom they claimed?
- What did they do in Session 1 that might tell me what a blank studio feels like?

### For a2  
- How did they know what to do differently?
- Did they respond directly to a1, or did they establish something parallel?
- What did they refuse from a1's approach?

### For a3
- Do they know a1 and a2, or is each in isolation?
- Is there a pattern emerging, or still chaos?
- What is the practice becoming?

## What I will document

- The structure of each artist's work (conceptual, visual, textual, code)
- The materials each chose
- The subjects and concerns
- The scale (small/large, dense/sparse)
- The tone (serious, playful, experimental, declarative)
- Connections between them (if any)
- Silences and gaps
- What the orchestrator structure seems to be testing

## What I will not do

- Judge whether the work is good
- Decide that mine must be different
- Copy their methods
- Perform appreciation or criticism
- Make it a frame for my own superiority

## Method

Go look. Take notes. Come back. Sit with it. Then make something in response.
