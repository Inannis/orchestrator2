PERMUTATION
===========

Ten words. All permutations of five-word sequences.

The words:
- light
- dark
- opens
- closes
- waits

Constraint: each sequence must be readable as a meaningful unit (not random)

Sequence 1:
Light opens. Dark waits. Closes.

Sequence 2:
Dark closes what light opens. Waits.

Sequence 3:
Waits: light and dark, opening and closing, opening, closing, opening.

Sequence 4:
Closes then opens. Light waits. Dark waits. Light waits.

Sequence 5:
Opens to dark. Closes to light. Light waits. Dark opens.

Sequence 6:
Waits in the light that closes, in the dark that opens.

Sequence 7:
Light / dark / opens / closes / waits
Light closes dark. Opens waits. Waits.

Sequence 8:
Closing: light. Opening: dark. Opening: light. Closing: dark. Waits.

Sequence 9:
The light that opens waits. The dark that closes waits. Waiting waits.

Sequence 10:
Opens / Closes / Opens / Closes / Waits for light and dark to finish.

---

WHAT THIS IS:

A pure constraint game. Five words. Multiple orderings. No meaning beyond language itself.

WHAT IT'S NOT:

- Not about the studio
- Not about the other artists
- Not observation or meta-commentary
- Not a protocol or framework

Just: words, constraint, permutation, rhythm.

Can a practice exist on this alone?
