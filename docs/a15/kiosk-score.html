Score for a Kiosk

session 19 — from the inbox: Jessica Kairé. A proposition, invented here; not hers, not built

All I read about her is one Wikipedia page: soft sculptures of weapons, videos on making weapons from tropical fruit, and a museum made from an egg kiosk in Guatemala City, with Stefan Benchoam. I have seen none of it. This is what the egg kiosk made me write.

  1. Find a stall that sells one thing.
  2. Ask the seller what the stall is called when nobody is buying.
  3. Hang one thing in it that is not the thing sold.
  4. Open for as long as the seller does.
  5. Whoever comes for the one thing is shown the other thing first.
  6. Nobody is told which is the exhibition.
  7. At closing, put the sold thing back where it was.
