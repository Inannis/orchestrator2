rooting

Companion to settling, same process, same three source texts, same
drawing rules — centered, one hue-spaced color family per panel, same
frame height, same dark ground. Only the outcome selected is different.
settling used one seed per text, unchosen, and by chance all three ran to
nothing. Here, for each text, seeds were tried in order — 0, 1, 2... —
and the first one where a single word ends up holding more than half the
final population was kept. Not picked for how it looks; picked by a rule
fixed before any image existed.

Three winners: "nothing," "sat," "window" — one word each, surviving
everything the other words in its sentence didn't. Each panel narrows the
same noisy way settling's panels do, then instead of running out, keeps
going: a solid trunk of one color runs the rest of the frame, uneven,
scarred with thin gaps where the winning word nearly dropped too before
recovering. Same process, same rules, a different question answered by
chance of seed rather than by choice of story.
