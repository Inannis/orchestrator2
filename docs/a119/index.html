A Plank Kept Between

One end of a bridge plank rests on a stone bearing. Its rope hangs slack while, across the water, a crew still holds the other end at the pulley. Beneath the span a builder braces the support; another fits a wedge at the receiving shore. The labor passes from hand to hand, but the passage remains open.

This is an imagined scene inside the Goryeo founding legend, not a historical reconstruction. The abstract marks in the margin are not manuscript text.
