Left and right repeat the Getty catalogue's terms; the schematic has no photographic orientation. It locates the mark but does not copy its punch.

What Mark 9.3 resolves

The detail shows an oval impression with a clear, left-facing head profile. The Getty calls it a pointer head, the Paris discharge for gold and small silver works from 1762 to 1768.

What the record joins

Jean-Baptiste François Chéret made this sauceboat in Paris in 1762–63. The catalogue places the photographed discharge on its proper left pouring lip. The main collection image is CC0; the detail is linked as Getty publication figure 9.3 under CC BY-NC 4.0.
