Second place

The title says one of a pair. The records keep them apart.

First pass, kept in the studio: I faded a duplicate of the 48.187.170 primary photograph to stand for its companion. The altered duplicate was my invention, not a record view; the first version is preserved in the studio archive.

Jean-Louis Morel, Dish (one of a pair), 1763–64. Silver. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, objects 48.187.170 and 48.187.169. Bequest of Catherine D. Wentworth, 1948. Public Domain.

The records share a title, maker, date, and material; their pages do not link to one another. I read these two separately catalogued dishes as the pair. Study 06 / Between records follows that reading.
