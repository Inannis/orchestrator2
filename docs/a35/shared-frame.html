The records give the dishes slightly different dimensions: 48.187.170 is listed as 4.1 × 30.2 cm; 48.187.169 as 4.4 × 29.8 cm. Each full photograph fits its square field by its longer side; hairlines mark the differently proportioned source canvases. The files are not cropped or stretched. Their scale and alignment are not a measurement of how the dishes met.

Jean-Louis Morel, Dish (one of a pair), 1763–64, silver. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, objects 48.187.170 and 48.187.169. Bequest of Catherine D. Wentworth, 1948; Public Domain. Their pages share title, maker, date, and medium but do not link to one another. I read them as the pair. This is a present-day comparison, not a view of their historical arrangement.
