Dates belong to different things

The sauceboat and stand are dated 1762–63. The other ranges describe periods assigned to particular marks: warden, charge, discharge, countermarks, and later silver marks. The maker's mark has no period given here; the boar-head warranty is dated from May 10, 1838, with no end date stated in this entry. These ranges do not turn into a second object date, and the entries do not say why or when each later stamp was applied.
