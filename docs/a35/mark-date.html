A mark and a date

The center details return beside each record's date.

The crops keep the shuffled order of Study 13 and use equal 480 × 480 pixel windows; the photographs are not shown to physical scale. The shared year scales run from 1690 to 1800. Rust marks a repeated heraldic device.

The two heraldic centers share the 1697–1704 range. The three plain centers fall between 1760 and 1764: .171 is dated 1760–61, the Morels .169/.170 are dated 1763–64. The device and matching date range lend separate support to reading .172/.173 together.

The plain centers do not distinguish the Morels from .171, whose scalloped rim is close to theirs. The .169/.170 pairing rests on the full photographs and their repeated title, maker, and date; these records do not cross-link. Source images are Public Domain.
