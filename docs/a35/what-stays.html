Every title repeats the same phrase, and every record names silver. Scrambled this way, the maker and date still recur in two sets. Jean-Louis Morel and 1763–64 recur in one set; the other two entries name no maker and repeat 1697–1704. The photographs and accession sequence are absent. I still read two pairs in these fields, though the museum entries do not link to one another.
