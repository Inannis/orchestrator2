Two component-specific classes and a separate maker's mark.

The grey bars report when the museum dates the object. Rust bars appear only when a record gives a period for a named discharge mark. The overlap is real, but the record fields answer different questions.

The Met dates .173 to 1697–1704; it does not call its profile a discharge, charge, or maker's mark. The teapot's circle is also unclassified. Nearby Paris objects give examples of dated discharges, including more than one category within a single multi-part object, but those records do not assign a class to the dish.
