Two clocks, no strike dates

The Getty assigns periods of use to the warden, charge, discharge, countermarks, restricted warranty, and auction standard. Those periods are not dates when each impression was made. The catalogue lists later marks on the base, but does not say when or why they were applied. The split scales preserve the long gap without claiming the base was unmarked during it.

Source and mark locations: Getty, French Silver, catalogue 9, especially the sauceboat and stand mark entries. The ranges plotted are mark-use periods stated by the catalogue, not observed application dates. Getty's close-up photographs are linked on Studies 34 and 35, not reproduced here.
