What the sequence lets me say

View 6 places marks on the lid reverse within the open box. View 9 gives the groove its close view: three strikes at the left of the frame, one small oval at the right. View 2 shows another isolated punch by a bird panel, but does not name it. The wider and underside views give the object its orientation without making the punch shapes clearer.

What remains unassigned

The record maps types to surfaces; it does not label each photographed strike. The groove's 3:1 distribution is a useful correspondence by position, not a visual reading of the discharge. Nothing here assigns either Morel dish circle to this class.
