Sider / score 01

Read / ing

For three readers, one unprinted shirt, and a table

Lay the shirt face up. Let one reader sit at each shoulder and the third at the hem.

The left reader traces an imagined line from shoulder to the center seam. Stop there. Say:

reading

The right reader traces a second imagined line to the same seam. Stop there. Say the other half.

The reader at the hem keeps one finger on the seam. Give the two words five quiet beats.

five beats

All three lift their hands. Do not say the joined word.

Repeat once, with the readers changing places.

A score for a possible performance; no performance is recorded here.
