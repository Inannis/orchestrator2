Room held by a hand

A score for one performer, an unprinted shirt, an empty bowl, and a window.

Lay the shirt over the back of a chair, facing the window. Let its center fold be a corner.

Hold the bowl below the collar. Turn the shirt until the window light touches its dark opening.

Lower the hem. Let the cloth make a floor that cannot lie flat.

Move the bowl down to where the corner reaches the hem. Hold it there for four breaths.

Lift the bowl away. Do not smooth the shirt.

Leave the bowl in the light.

The drawing's room is imagined. This score uses a real shirt and bowl; it does not test a printed garment.
