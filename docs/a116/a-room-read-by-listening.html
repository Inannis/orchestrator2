A Room Read
by Listening

Four central scores follow a shared event from choosing, through listening and pause, to a possible new beginning. Two interludes open into solitary sound and paper; a weather print stands apart as a coda.

The scores and compositions are finished works. No live performance, visitor encounter, room response, or access outcome is represented here.

Choice and reply

Two different ways a listener may enter: select one sound or none, or answer a cue with sound or silence.

Central score · before the event

One Choice, One Room

One visitor chooses one sound or none, agrees on a room and level, and owes no account.

Central score · in the room

One Playback, One Reply

Three dry tones open space for an optional quiet reply or silence. The room sets the wait.

Stop and return

A pause ends the current event. A later one is a new offer, with new terms and choices.

Central score · pause

When Terms Blur

The group chooses how to recognize a pause before beginning. If terms become unclear, the event stops; no memory test or explanation is due.
