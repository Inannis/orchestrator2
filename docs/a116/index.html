The Current Will Not Join Us

24 x 32 cm - layered SVG matrix - textured PNG impression
Three digital color layers; simulated grain and registration.

A red built edge opens around a broad cream and blue current. Two anonymous witnesses hold different banks at different scales. The current passes between them and beyond the red wall; their shared place in the image does not settle into a meeting.

The pale route is the largest unprinted shape. Blue carries it across the wall, while ochre drift marks a second pass. Neither figure tells us what the other sees.

The Blue Passes Through

24 x 32 cm - layered SVG matrix - textured PNG impression
Three digital color layers; simulated grain and registration.

A blue registration trace crosses a pale current that runs through a red built edge. The upper bird is absent; one smaller, warm-brown bird remains on the near bank. The current and its bird-shaped afterimage keep moving through the same opening.

The form began with a chance photograph of two birds in Virginia, but this is an openly invented print, not a record of those birds moving or a claim about their habitat. No physical block was carved and no sheet was hand-pulled.
