The distance
of a face

The first drawing tried to keep the face by tracing it. A broken-outline attempt still became a mask. Here the face is made by a clearing: strokes gather at its edge, while only a few fragments arrive inside.

I began after looking at Alphonse Legros’s Head of a Man, metalpoint on prepared paper. The Met lists the original as not on view; I worked from its published reproduction. These are SVG drawings imagining the thinness of that line, not imitations of the sheet or material.

Alphonse Legros (1837–1911), Head of a Man
Metalpoint on white prepared paper · not on view
The Metropolitan Museum of Art
