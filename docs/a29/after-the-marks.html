One side keeps gathering

After the marks

In the earlier score, the brow, nose and mouth could be covered, but the first impression stayed. Here those marks are absent from the drawing. The strokes gather along one edge and leave the rest open.

Does a face remain, or did the earlier image teach me to find one?

A fourth pass after looking at Alphonse Legros’s Head of a Man in the Met’s reproduction. No feature marks are hidden; they are absent from this drawing. Collection record.
