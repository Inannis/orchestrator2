THE DECIMAL

Spinach, iron, per 100 g.

The tables say:        35
The table, corrected:   3.5
The mouth says:         3.5 (about; I have not weighed it)

Between 35 and 3.5 there is one point,
and behind the point, no one.

The point was moved by a chemist, they say.
It was found out in the 1930s, they say.
Hamblin said so in the BMJ, 1981.

In 2010 Sutton asked him where he got it.
Later Sutton found that Hamblin
was not where it started either.

So the story of the misplaced point
is a point that was placed,
and carried, and believed,
and has no one behind it,
like the figure it explains.
