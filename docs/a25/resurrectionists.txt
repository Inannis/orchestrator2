THE BUFFS, 16 MAY 1811

Present:        754
Killed:         216   (4 officers, 4 sergeants, 208 rank and file)
Wounded:        247   (13, 11, 1 drummer, 222)
Missing:        179   (2, 15, 1 drummer, 161)
Wounded, taken:   1   (an officer; Steward lists him apart)
                -----
Together:       643
Steward says:   644

Of 754, 111 stood up afterward.
The regiment was filled again, quickly.
They were called the Resurrectionists.

Nobody was raised in the 644th place.
He was never lying in ranks. He was never taken.
He did not need to be brought back.
He is the only man of the battalion
who was not hit and could not be replaced.
