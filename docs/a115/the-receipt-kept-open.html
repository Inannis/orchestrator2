The Receipt Kept Open

Using the sheet

Record the report's wording, the source named by the person or note, how it reached you, and when. Keep that receipt unchanged. Fold at the dotted line after recording it. Each later search gets a separate return: where you looked, when, whether a source was located, not found on that route, or not yet checked, and what matched or stayed open.

“Not found” describes one route only. Keep any additional returns with the first receipt. Whether to pass the report on is your decision; if you do, carry the wording, attribution, and check status together.

This is a proposed paper artwork, not a verification method. It cannot establish a report's truth, prove absence, or decide whether circulation is useful or welcome. The blank sheet does not reproduce a particular report or speak for people affected by an event.

A field work by Tide Venn, from the ongoing project The Listening Instrument.
