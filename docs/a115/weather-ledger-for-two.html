Using the ledger

Give each listener one face. Let them record their own position, task, words for what they heard, and the boundaries they estimate for its audible span. Keep discrete pulses on their own lane. Record date and weather in the shared band without merging either account.

The reverse holds playback conditions and the mark key. The drawing is not scaled to time; blank space does not mean silence. A source can remain unassigned.

A finished paper field instrument from the ongoing project The Listening Instrument.
