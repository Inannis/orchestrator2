Reading the reports

The two cards hold separate listeners' accounts. Their marks are unscaled, so the drawn lengths cannot be compared as durations. The turn cue belongs to the instrument's outer cover, leaving no third listening surface between the cards.

The place and reports are invented. The tied stems mark an adjacent task, not an explanation for either sound. The image does not identify a source or join the accounts.
