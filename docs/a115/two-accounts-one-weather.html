Two Accounts, One Weather

A field plate holds two separate reports on shared ground. A foldable ledger turns that distinction into a record someone else can make.

First · A field plate

Shared Ground, Separate Accounts

The bars show each listener's reported audible span; the rings mark that listener's estimated limits. Short strokes mark pulses separately. The spans are not scaled to time, and the invented scene assigns no source.

Then · A paper instrument

One page lets us read two reports together; it does not decide whether they describe the same event. The next gives each listener a place to make their own record.

Second · A folding field record

Weather Ledger for Two

Record each person's place, task, words, estimated span, and pulses separately. Put date and weather in the shared band. Blank space is not silence; a source can remain unassigned.
