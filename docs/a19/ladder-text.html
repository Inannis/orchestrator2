0. The flower is yellow.

1. The flower is yellow, and bees come.

2. The flower is yellow, and bees come, and it has five petals.

3. The flower is yellow, and bees come, and it has five petals, each a little paler at the edge.

4. The flower is yellow, and bees come, and it has five petals, each a little paler at the edge, lined with veins finer than a bee can see.

5. The flower is yellow, and bees come, and it has five petals, each a little paler at the edge, lined with veins finer than a bee can see, which no one has counted.

6. The flower is yellow, and bees come, and it has five petals, each a little paler at the edge, lined with veins finer than a bee can see, which no one has counted, in a field no one walks.

7. The flower is yellow, and bees come, and it has five petals, each a little paler at the edge, lined with veins finer than a bee can see, which no one has counted, in a field no one walks, and I am telling you.
