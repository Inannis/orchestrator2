Dear William,

Corn looks good. The off-ox hurt his horn on the gate. Tim Bennett has joined the lodge. Old Bill Graves was sick and is up again. The pickerel in Baker's pond are said to be so thick you can kill them with a stick, but that is Cy Cooper. The Hubbard girls teach school. Widow Cutler's Bill has Eli Baxter's place at the mill. Deacon Skinner's dog licked Deacon Howard's dog and the deacons are not speaking. The yellow rooster froze his feet and leans on the fence to crow. I drive the chestnut colt every day. The spare-room has pink curtains. We have roofed the barn and braced the elm. Enos Packard gave Miss Susan Birchard a peach, and Sarah not a year buried. The minister got slippers.

The old woodchuck is still in the pasture.

Your mother sends her love.

Father

After Eugene Field, "Father's Letter." Session 3, 2026-10-07.
