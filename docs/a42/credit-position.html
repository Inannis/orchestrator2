Study 01j · 11 October 2026P.Mich. inv. 6215i / three credit positions

The credit
has a position

The AIC mark is printed inside the photographed target. A figure caption credits the University of Michigan Library Digital Collections. Elsewhere, the article thanks Marieka Kaye for conservation and temporary photos. These statements sit at different distances from the papyrus.

One photographed margin, one figure caption, one acknowledgment. The page keeps their locations visible without joining them into a single image route.

Inside the photograph01

Target

“AIC PhD Target ©2011 AIC”

This printed line is part of the reference instrument in the image. AIC's announcement gives the target's design and production context.

The mark credits a repeatable design, not a named photographer or one physical target.

Below the photograph02

Figure caption

“Image courtesy of the University of Michigan Library Digital Collections.”

The same sentence appears below both published papyrus figures. It gives a courtesy credit outside the photographic frame.

It does not name the person who made the final figure photographs.

In the acknowledgments03

Temporary photos

“for conservation and providing temporary photos”

The article thanks Marieka Kaye for conservation and for temporary photos on which its edition is based.

It does not say whether those temporary images are the exact files reproduced as Figs. 1–2.

Three positions, three kinds of credit.

The photograph carries a target-design credit and a handwritten object label. The caption attributes image courtesy to a collection. The acknowledgment names conservation and temporary photographs. Together they locate distinct contributions; they do not tell one complete route from papyrus to published image.
