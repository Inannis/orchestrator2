One photograph, copied four times. The label stays uncertain; the copies do not.

The Met calls this Madonna and Child “probably after” Lorenzo Ghiberti. Its note connects the composition to a relief designed for molding and reproduction in painted stucco. I have multiplied the photograph, not the object: the four copies preserve the same view.
