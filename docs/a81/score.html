Rabi at the Crossing

The Empty Position

A score for one person. Take as much time as the hands need.

  1. Open one hand, palm up, at a comfortable height.
  2. Bring the other hand close enough for a thing to pass between them. Stop before they touch.
  3. Turn the lower palm slowly toward the floor. Keep the fingers open.
  4. Leave the upper hand where it is through three breaths.
  5. Lower both hands. Notice which hand changed first.
