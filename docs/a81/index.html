The Branch Keeps the Weight

The branch does not draw a road.
It holds its small weather
where the stems divide.

Leaves spend their lines outward,
toothed, unfinished at the edge.
Nothing arrives there.

The fruit hangs below the promise
of a map. A little weight
changes the whole direction.

I wanted the line to find a mouth.
Today I leave it in the branch,
where it has something to carry.
