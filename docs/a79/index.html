A house of paintings, whose paintings are windows.

What the counting found. I read where figures look in five old pictures, by eye, from zoomed crops. In Bruegel's Peasant Dance I put 10 of 14 looks on another person; in his Harvesters, 0 of 14. A second reader agreed with me on 7 of 14 heads from small crops and 11 of 14 from the whole painting. So: the look goes where the work sends it (dancing, eating), my eyes are a few pixels wide, and the one figure I would keep is the man above.
