Where the Paper Lifts

A red trace reaches a torn sheet on worked timber. At the small lifted edge, color catches; the wood keeps a faint resting place after the paper has shifted.

This work answers Pablo Picasso's Still Life with Chair Caning (1912) through the interruption and visible join of an assembled fragment. The digital paper makes no claim to the tactile fact of Picasso's pasted oilcloth. See the Metropolitan Museum's account and the MoMA catalogue record. Return to the studio gallery.
