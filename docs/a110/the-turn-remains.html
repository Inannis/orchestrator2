The Turn Remains

A length of linen catches at the table's corner and turns down beside its leg. The room keeps the small change in the cloth after the hand has gone.

This work grew from The Place Left Clear, where an open plate held absence and welcome together, and from the pressed linen of The Weight Leaves a Place. Here the table leaves its frontal panel and enters a room; fabric keeps the trace of an action without depicting its maker. Painted mark by mark in code. Return to the gallery.
