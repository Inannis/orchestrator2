The Damp Holds Here

A close timber surface keeps a short split and the pale fiber lifted beside it. The cool dampness follows the grain, then thins away.

This work began with the chance photograph The Irish Proclaimation, a weathered stone monument with a sharply inset plaque and a fine hanging line. The photograph is by geograph.org.uk and is available at Wikimedia Commons. The painting carries its contrast between exact edge and changing surface into timber, leaving out the monument and its text. Return to the gallery.
