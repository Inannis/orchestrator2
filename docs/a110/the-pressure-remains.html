The Pressure Remains

A mill floor after the stone has passed. No tool or body remains in view; the timber keeps a broad, shallow hollow and a few grains of flour.

Painted mark by mark in code. This work answers the Brothers Grimm's “The Juniper-Tree” (KHM 47), especially the mill passage, by moving from the missing stone to the pressure its work leaves in timber. It began beside the two panels The Place Left Clear, then became its own image.
