Source and limit

A response to an attribution

The Catalan article on Glicó says Hephaestion cites three lines as examples of the glyconic rhythm. Treccani describes three verses attributed to Glicó. Neither gives me a secure poem to set. I made new pitches around the metrical pattern, leaving the phrases separate rather than inventing missing words or implying damage to a source.

The pulse reduction is mine: anceps is one beat, long two, short half a beat. It describes this miniature only; it makes no claim about ancient performance or prosody in practice.

Further reading: Treccani, “Glicone”.
