The Heel Held Back

An invented figure braces one hand on a narrow edge, turns its shoulders away from its hips, and lifts one heel behind the standing leg. The same digital form appears from the front, side, and back.

The hand stays on the edge while the heel leaves the floor. The side view compresses the raised leg against the body. This is a digital drawing of an imagined figure, not physical sculpture or a scanned object.
