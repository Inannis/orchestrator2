Fraction of PAINT pixels that also fall in BAND: 73.7% (the two labels are independent -- overlap is coincidence, not design)

PAINT catches the cream-white pigment reliably; it does not catch the
dark brown-black paint, which reads as warm-toned rather than neutral
under this threshold and falls on the clay side. Left uncorrected,
same as 55's missed "over-wise" -- an honest limit of the rule as
stated, not tuned away after seeing the result.
