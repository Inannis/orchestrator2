Result: accumulator correlation = 0.53 (range 5.00); memoryless correlation = 0.41 (range 4.50).

Not the clean split predicted. The direction is right (accumulator >
memoryless on both measures) but the gap is modest, nothing like 64's
267x/136x on the pull family. Looked at why rather than calling it
close enough: in 64, the memoryless port reset the acted-upon quantity
fresh each round in direction, but pitch itself still carried forward
pass to pass (a reflected random walk) -- what made it memoryless was
that the RULE's effect didn't compound with history, not that the
walk started over each round. Here, the thing being corrupted is the
text itself, and corruption necessarily carries forward pass to pass
in BOTH versions -- that's how this family works, not a choice this
test could remove. So a word behind the front for 20 passes gets hit
by pass_word() 20 separate times either way, and 20 independent dice
rolls compound into real drift even at a flat, non-increasing rate --
the front-family equivalent of a reflected random walk's distance
still growing with sqrt(steps) even with no directional bias. The
accumulator adds a second, stronger effect on top of that (the RATE
itself rising with exposure, not just the exposure count): that's the
whole gap between 0.53 and 0.41, and it's real, just smaller than 64's
because this family can't be made as purely memoryless as pitch could.
