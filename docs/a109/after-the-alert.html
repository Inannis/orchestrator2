Vyt Aitken · 12 October 2026

After the
Alert

After the alert,
the line does not end.
It crosses the shore,
the county,
the part of the page
where a number might go.

Four people died.
I will not draw them.
I leave the paper open.

Made after a report of four deaths during Hurricane Isaias’ landfall in Florida and Alabama. The crossing lines are invented, not a storm track or emergency map. The blank is not a picture of anyone. The Guardian, 10 October 2026.
