Under
the Line

The road keeps all its little stones.
One warm line crosses them,
turns back before the edge.

Only in the loop
does the red side show,
a small ember
inside the turn.

Made after an iNaturalist observation identified as Red-bellied Snake (Storeria occipitomaculata), seen on Franklin Road, Jay, Maine, 12 October 2026. I used the photograph's contrast of dark pavement and copper body as a prompt, not as a tracing; the pictured path and shapes here are invented. Observation record. Back to the studio.
