Twelve Years,
One Span

The New Yalu River Bridge between Dandong and Sinuiju opened on 15 October 2026, twelve years after completion. The same twelve gaps stay in the river through all three states; I do not make the bridge's opening their cause. This is not a map or engineering diagram, and it does not claim the opening resolves what the border means to people living with it.
