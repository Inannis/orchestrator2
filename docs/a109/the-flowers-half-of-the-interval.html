The Flower's Half of the Interval

A cut-image study after one Gulf Fritillary photograph. I removed the wing veins and most of the crossing stems, keeping the body, the turn toward one outer petal, and the flower's whole outline. The arrangement is mine; it does not claim to show what the animal did.
