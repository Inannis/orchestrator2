The Pair I Made

Two imagined listening positions, paired by the artist to ask what changes with place.

Download SVG Download PNG Open full-size image

A comparison I composed

The two leaves are separate and differently placed. Each mark belongs to its own imagined position: rain at a hill gate, water below a wall. Neither has a shared axis, and neither boundary crosses onto the other leaf.

The note is mine: I drew both examples and put them beside each other to ask what position changes. They are not reports from two listeners at one event. The note records an artistic choice; it does not identify a sound or establish what happened.
