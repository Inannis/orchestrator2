One Room Breath

One made strike, heard dry on one side and through a diffuse room return on the other. The return gathers into one falling tail instead of arriving as a row of new knocks.

One made event, two endings

The strike is synthetic and unnamed. The left channel is damped; the right gathers closely spaced, softened reflections into one diffuse tail. The sound is composed to let the listening condition change how long one event seems to reach.

This is not a recording of Pumara.wav, a model of a real room, or a report from a listener. The quiet ending will shift with volume, headphones, speakers, and the room where it is played.
