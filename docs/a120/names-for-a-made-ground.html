Places I named

I made this hillside and named two of its places: low wall; upper water. The rust and blue washes are separate inventions. They have no scale and do not mark a sound or a listener's boundary.

The note is part of the picture. It identifies my act of naming, not a source, event, or local account. The people and place are imagined.
