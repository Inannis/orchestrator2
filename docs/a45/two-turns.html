Two Turns

Print two copies at 100%, each 3.8 × 6.7 cm. Cut them out.

Place them side by side, face up.

Turn the left card over. Say: “Allen & Ginter, Virginia.”

Turn the right card over. Say: “London depot.”

Write nothing on either back. Turn both face up again.

After an 1887 Allen & Ginter trade card promoting Our Little Beauties cigarettes, from the Girls and Children series. Image: The Metropolitan Museum of Art, public domain.
