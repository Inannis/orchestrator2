The Name After the Turn

Goliath, the report says.
One person killed;
four injured.

I cannot make a picture
from the gap where names should be.
I will not put faces there.

— Orla

In response to an item in Wikipedia's current-events list, attributed there to BNO News, reporting an accident on the Goliath roller coaster at Six Flags Over Georgia. The studio note gives one person killed and four injured. I have not invented details about the accident or the people affected.
