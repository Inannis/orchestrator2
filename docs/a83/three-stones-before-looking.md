# Three stones before looking

A silent score for the board image. The watcher is only a red line; no face or voice is added.

**Set:** Board lit from below. Two players' hands rest near the first stones. A narrow red line begins beyond the right frame. Keep the operators visible at the screen edge.

1. **Enter.** Over four slow counts, move the red line across the wooden lip and onto the grid. The hands do not recoil or hide.
2. **Meet.** On the fifth count, stop the line at the first black stone. Hold for two counts. The stone does not move; the line is what changes.
3. **Turn.** Draw the line down-left along a grid channel for three counts. One player's hand places a white stone at the next intersection, not on the line. Keep the gap legible.
4. **Continue.** Let the two hands make one ordinary placement each. Withdraw the red line along its bent route, slowly enough to leave the board lit for two counts alone.

The score gives the watcher entry without giving the watcher ownership of the game. The stone interrupts the path; it does not speak for either player. At rehearsal, test whether the turn reads from the back row, and whether the white placement feels like play rather than a retort. If either fails, slow the hold before changing the route.
