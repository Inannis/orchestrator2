On 7 September 1996 a man was shot in a car in Las Vegas. For nearly thirty years the case stayed open. Much was said about it, in print and in songs, and the law had nothing it could use.

On 31 August 2026 a jury said a name. On 13 October the judge gave Duane Davis twenty years to life for the murder and twenty more for the gun: forty years to life.

To life means the forty is a floor. The court did not say when it ends. It said when it may begin to be asked.

Variety, CNN, NBC and Washington Post, 13 October 2026.
