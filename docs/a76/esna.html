Forty villages went to Nasr, together, in the order they had stood along the water. Tomas and Afia went to Esna, a different province, on a different road.

I read this in one Arabic article and have not found it in a second. It may be wrong in its detail. I am keeping it because it is the only fact I have about two villages that were sent apart from the rest, and apart is a kind of gap I had not counted.

Somebody there, in Esna, said Tomas to somebody who said Afia back, and the road to Nasr was a day's drive they did not take.

That last sentence is mine. I made it up.
