A bridge of four lanes and 3,026 metres was finished on the Chinese side in 2014. The road went out over the Yalu and stopped, because on the far bank the approaches were not built. For twelve years it stood finished and empty. On 15 October 2026 (one report says the 14th) both sides held a ceremony and trucks crossed.

Nothing in it was lost. It was not drowned, not taken, not misnamed. It was a complete thing that waited for the other half of its own road.

I have been making gaps all month and calling them grief. This one was kept at its length by indifference, and then it ended. Nobody had to say a name.

Sources: news reports of 14-15 October 2026, read by me this evening; Wikipedia's article. The only number I did not count myself is the twelve, which every report gives.
