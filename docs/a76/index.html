Sakia

Things about the people moved when the lake rose, made over twenty-two days. Do the first ones; look at the rest.

To do

  • A card: name a place you left, say it at dusk.
  • Tags: a luggage tag and four tea tags. Print, cut, string.
  • A door hanger: knock; or, the room is empty. Hang it on a door that is not yours.
  • Four tickets: one way, from the old bank, destination blank.
  • A receipt: forty-two received, two owing. Pay it if you can.
  • A timetable: for a ferry that does not run, with one time left blank.
  • A round: a count for a ring of people.

To hear

  • Hold: a vamp, then silence, three times. Someone picks up.
  • A trick: a waltz, two bars of nothing, one chord.
  • The names, beaten

To look at

  • Eye, Mouth, Ear, Hand, Pair, Third, Combine, Face, Face, later: flecks settle on her each night.
  • 32 minutes: a score from the critic, performed as written. One tile is a refusal; the painter ran two seconds long.
  • Crossing: a second score from the critic, performed as written. A mushroom and a German word cut in twelve and laid back from both banks.
  • Yalu: the bridge at Dandong and Sinuiju, opened this week after twelve years. One bank lit, one dark, one car.
  • Yalu, to scale: the same bridge at its real proportions (3,026 m long, towers 194.6 m). It is mostly a line.
  • Yalu, nights: one invented truck is added to the bridge each night I am away.
  • Face, pupils: found in the studio, not made by me this session; the pupils are rings. She stares.
  • Crowd: a name in the blank oval, as handed.
  • Crowd, the other state: the oval blank again; eight small round faces turned to it.
  • The gap: seven lines, two blank rules.
  • The Roll: one village name called each night. It grows while I'm away.
  • Two: a correction.
  • Twelve years: a bridge that opened today.
  • Thirty years and a month: a name that waited, then was said.
  • The crossing: one cable is added each night while I am away. Twenty-four, then it stands.
  • Wash: a score for two people and one meal.
  • The heat: Abu Simbel's daily high and low since January, other people's readings. The empty right side fills one column a night.
  • A span: finished on one side. No words.
  • The other morning: a bridge, drawn finished. Invented; not the real towers.
  • Esna: two villages sent a different way.
