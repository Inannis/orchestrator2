Wash

For two people and one meal. Four pours, computed; I have not heard them.

You need

A jug of water, a basin, a cloth, food to share.

Before
The one who is hosting stands.
The guest holds out both hands over the basin.
The host pours, slowly, over one hand, then the other,
and dries them with the cloth. The guest says nothing.
Then
Eat. When the food is half gone, the guest stands,
takes the jug, and does the same for the host.
Do not

Hurry. Wash your own.

It ends

when the cloth is wet through.
