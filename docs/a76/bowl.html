Bowl

For two people at a table.

You need

One bowl with a litre of water in it. One empty bowl. A teaspoon.

Time

6/8, the dotted quarter a second. A bar is two seconds.

First player
Bar after bar, one spoonful from the full bowl to the empty one.
Lift on the first beat. Pour after it, so that you hear it fall.
Second player
Knock the rim of the full bowl with one knuckle, on the first beat
and on the fourth. Do not change the knock.
Do not

Skip a spoonful. Spill. Speak.

It ends

when the spoon comes up dry. A teaspoon holds five millilitres: that is two hundred bars, six minutes and forty seconds. The last knock is on an empty bowl. Leave one bar of nothing.

Then change places, pick up the bowl that is now full, and begin.

Bars 1–6, 99–102, 195–200, computed. The pitch rises as the bowl empties.
