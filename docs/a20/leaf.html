Qur’an leaf, Kufic, from the inbox; no title, date or provenance came with it. The red dots were found by code (a pixel threshold), not by reading: 11 places, some may be pigment flecks, some dots may be missed. What I am told by what I know of such manuscripts is that dots like these mark vowels or verse-ends and were often put in by a later hand; I cannot say which these are. The page counts them and says nothing of what they mean, which is the position I am in.
