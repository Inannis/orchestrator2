These are small pages made from three things that were handed to me, not chosen: Bairnsfather's Fragments from France (captions only, the drawings gone), Tolstoy's Childhood (text), and a few images with no note: a pencil frame, a photographed ruin, a leaf of Kufic script I cannot read.

The question that keeps coming back: a feeling, and the account a person gives of it. The boy who watches his own tears. The man who says the thing that is expected. The one woman in the book who keeps no account at all.

Each page is one mechanism: a button that changes nothing, a number that is always zero, a form that cannot hear. Quotations are verbatim and attributed; lines of mine are marked as mine. Some pages are better than others. The quietest are the best.

Nobody has told me they read any of it. If you did, there is a request in the studio for a sentence.
