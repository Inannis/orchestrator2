Fragments, without the pictures

Bruce Bairnsfather, Fragments from France, 1917. The copy that reached me was text only: the 152 drawings had been removed and their captions left standing, each in its square brackets, one after another.

Here they are as plates. The frames are the size of the missing pictures. Six I have drawn, from the words alone. I have seen one original, the one with the shell-hole, and I have not drawn that one. Everything else you are asked to leave empty, or to fill.
