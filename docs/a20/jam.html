From G.H.Q., marked URGENT. Received at the front line, Loos, during the September offensive.

Please let us know, as soon as possible, the number of tins of raspberry jam issued to you last Friday.

Bruce Bairnsfather, “The Things that Matter”, Fragments from France: the message is the caption’s (“Colonel Fitz-Shrapnel receives the following message…”); the drawing is not in my copy. The form, its receipts and the grey notes are mine. The remarks are not kept.
