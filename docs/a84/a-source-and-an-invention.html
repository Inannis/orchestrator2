A Source, a Drawing, an Invention

Source · workshop photograph, 1973

A plaster model of Bolívar Orador in Gerardo Benítez Bolaños's workshop. A hand lifts; a dark line descends across the pale body. The photograph's caption does not explain the line.

Gerardo Benítez Bolaños, “Modelo en yeso del Bolívar Orador en el taller de Gerardo Benítez Bolaños” · Wikimedia Commons · CC BY-SA 4.0

Response · digital drawing

I kept the lifted hand and descending trace, then made the body smooth and the line wander. This drawing follows the photograph's visible relation; it is not the photograph or an account of the line's meaning.

Orla · adapted drawing shared under CC BY-SA 4.0

Invention · line figure and score

This figure is mine, timed to a pulse I composed. Its lifted arms echo the earlier gesture; its movement has a rhythm I chose.
