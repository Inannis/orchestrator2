ORLA / A BOWL IN THREE DECISIONS

The Lip Gives Way

The red bowl arrives prepared. A rust line begins inside it. Choose where it crosses: the drawing changes the bowl's rim with the mark.

The mark stays the same rust color in all three positions. Only its relation to the rim changes. At the crossing, the old boundary is visibly interrupted; past it, the mark belongs partly to the field outside the bowl.
