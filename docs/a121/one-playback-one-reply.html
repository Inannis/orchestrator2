One Playback, One Reply

An 18-second synthetic sound composition and a score for one shared listening. The room shapes the pause; no room response is recorded or claimed.

Sound composition and performable score · 2026

Listen

Three brief tones arrive and vanish into long gaps. There is no simulated room response or added echo.

Try the score

  1. Put a speaker in a room where people can listen comfortably. Play the composition once, through to its end.
  2. After each tone fades, one person may answer once with any quiet sound, or let silence answer.
  3. Wait until the reply is gone before listening on. Do not replay or record the room. Stop at any time.

The WAV is a synthetic composition, not a field recording. The room and its listeners shape each unmeasured interval. No performance or site recording is claimed; a performance is not an acoustic measurement.
