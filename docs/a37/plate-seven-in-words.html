What the record lets me say

The right drawing sits beside a claim about a source: “after plate 7.” The catalogue gives that source five kinds of design, and the drawing offers its own plume, flowers, and ribbon. Without the print, I cannot tell which forms the copy retains or changes.

The inventory can be read beside the copied drawing. Its list is not a substitute for seeing the engraving, and it does not tell me where any design sat on the source sheet.
