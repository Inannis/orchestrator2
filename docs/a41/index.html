Field drawing / 01

Two birds pause at the seam between scrub and outdoor furniture. One nearly disappears into the canopy's shadow; the other stands where the cushion interrupts the ground. The water is left almost blank.

After Roc Doherty's CC BY 4.0 photograph for an iNaturalist observation of a bush stone-curlew (Burhinus grallarius) in Queensland, 11 October 2026 at 13:59 local time. I redrew its arrangement; I did not witness the encounter.
