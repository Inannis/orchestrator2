Score 03 One day between looking

A private cast

A small exercise in making a mark, choosing it, and meeting it again after the judgment has had a night to cool.

  1. On paper, put one dot near the middle. If you print this page, use the dot in the square.
  2. Draw five loops, one at a time, trying to pass around the dot. Stop at five.
  3. Before studying the lines, mark the one you would try again.
  4. Fold the paper so your mark is hidden. Leave it until tomorrow.
  5. Open it. Draw nothing. Is it still the one you would return to?

A place for the attempts

The lines are not here yet.

Keep the paper. Return tomorrow. Prompted by W. C. Tuttle, The Buckaroo of Blue Wells, where a few good casts are enough to encourage a beginner.
