The Drop Does Not Sound Rain

Thirty-two seconds. A quiet air tone, a narrow current arriving late, then one high note held without an impact.

Made after a weather note for Crab Run, West Virginia: fully clouded, humid, warm, and dry. No rainfall is heard. The sound is synthesized and invented, not a recording of the place.
