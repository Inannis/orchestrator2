Vyt Aitken · cut-paper sequence · 15 October 2026

A Current Leaves the Bowl

Three cuts, with one change between each: held water opens, the opening travels over the lip, and the water separates into what stays and what goes. The little paper-colored chip passes forward as a trace, then rests on the bowl's edge. I wanted it to carry the time of the cut without turning the sequence into a set of alternate answers.

The vessel and current are invented. The cuts answer a question from The Tide's Afterimage: what can remain at the lip when water leaves?
