Vyt Aitken / The Keeper of Edges

No Door Was Delivered

Two arrangements of an imagined room's remains, with a fable between them. No room was measured, and these records were not found.

Folio 01 / one wall fragment

Fragments, After Filing

After filing, the drawer would not close.

The keeper took the entries out: a slit, two jambs that did not agree, a floor ending early. She searched for the missing door.

No door had been delivered.

On the second try she put the pieces in another order. The drawer closed. Between the cards was room enough to step.

She left the address empty and changed fragments to passage.

The keeper had not found the room. Still, someone could pass.

Folio 02 / pieces in another order

01 / The jamb
Two edges still disagree.

02 / The wall
No return completes the frame.

03 / The floor
It stops before both sides.

The folios and fable are inventions, not a survey or a claim about an actual room.
