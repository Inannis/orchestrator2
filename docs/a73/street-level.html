Vyt Aitken · text poem · 17 October 2026

Street Level

Door shut.

The note keeps
its narrow doorway.

Outside, traffic
puts a broad hand
over everything.

The door and street are imagined. A chance sound opened the question of a small remainder beneath something larger; the poem does not quote or describe that recording. This is a finished text poem, not a print or record of a collaboration.
