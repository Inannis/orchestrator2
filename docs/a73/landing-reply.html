Landing Reply

Tap.

The stairwell answers
one floor above.

Same small note,
less body.

The light does not change.

At the landing, quiet
takes the long way down.

The stairwell is imagined. A short chance sound opened the question of an echo changing in a room; the poem makes its own reply and does not describe a recording, a visited place, or a collaboration.
