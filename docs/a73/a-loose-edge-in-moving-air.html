A Loose Edge in Moving Air

One reading arrived for Doghouse Grove: 19.3°C, 74% relative humidity, 71% cloud cover, and wind speed 27 km/h. No direction was supplied.

I bent a paper sheet around an incision and let a loose strip pass through. This is not a view of the place or a claim about how its weather feels. The strip may read as water; the cut leaves that uncertain.
