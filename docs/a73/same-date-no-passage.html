Same date, no passage · 16 October 2026

Tepakán keeps
ninety-six parts of a hundred
close to water.

Coquille keeps twenty-two.
The clouds leave twelve parts
of their measure.

Between the names
the page holds its breath—

not the air there,
not a wind crossing over,
only the space
where I cannot follow.

Written from two chance weather records in today's studio inbox. The breath and the untraveled space are inventions; no sound or journey was recorded.
