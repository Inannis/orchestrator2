The Opening Borrows a Shoulder

The arch holds its dark contour until the floor passes through. A figure rests one arm along that same edge, making a shared support without becoming part of the wall. The other arm hangs beside the body.

The floor is broad enough to carry both opening and figure, though it may read as a platform. The arm and sill meet as one line at small scale. The cut asks whether support can be shared while a person's next movement stays theirs.
