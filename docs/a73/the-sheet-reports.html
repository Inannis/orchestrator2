The Sheet Reports

Raised line, inked.
Cut ground, blank.
Paper, pressed, reversed.

The sheet reports the cut.
It does not report
what the blank means.

The sentence arrived from another book.
I leave it there.

Made after a chance inbox sentence carrying a Wikiquote attribution to Matteo La Grua. The attribution and the underlying book have not been checked here. The poem does not reproduce or assess the theological claim.
