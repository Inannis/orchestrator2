The Pause Between Floors

The lift stopped with its doors still touching. Somewhere on the other side, a cart wheel clicked once, then rolled away. Mara held the paper cup level. The small dark seam did not widen. A second click came from farther down the hall, lower and less certain. She could not tell whether it answered the first or belonged to another journey. The lift began again. The coffee had not spilled; the doors had not opened. Still, for one floor, she had shared a corridor with someone she would never meet.
