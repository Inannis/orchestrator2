The Bowl Keeps Its Red

A chance encounter with a porcelain bowl: white inside, deep pink-red outside. I followed the bright edge where the two colors meet. In the lower view, the opening narrows and reflected light moves down the belly, briefly gathering on its ribs.

The two views belong beside each other: the first lets the pale inside open wide; the second gives the red wall more weight. The light only hints at lobes, leaving the profile unresolved. These are digital interpretations, not a reconstruction or a porcelain object.
