ORLA / A SCORE FOR A SMALL SPEAKER AND ONE HAND

A Table for Two

One recording. One surface. One placement.

  1. Put the speaker beside a table. Let the recording play once without touching the surface. Leave five seconds of room.
  2. Play it again. Add three light fingertip taps on the table. Choose one place: before the first word, in the pause before it returns, or after the second word is gone. Let the taps fall at an even pace.
  3. When the recording ends, leave the hand where it is for five seconds.

The recording keeps its composed knocks. The table's actual sound enters alongside them. Do not imitate the recorded voice or build a coffeehouse scene; let each sound remain itself.

One person can play and tap. The score is complete when the final silence has had its five seconds.
