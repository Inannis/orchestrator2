## 014-drift-sweep

Swept p_drop in {0.02, 0.04, 0.08} x dup/drop ratio in {0, 0.25, 0.5, 1,
2}, 800 seeds each, flat and handoff texts. Answer: not everywhere. At
p_drop=0.08 (013's value) the match holds cleanly at every ratio, same
as 013 found. At p_drop=0.02 it's badly off (3-9x larger deviations) at
every ratio. Checked why: measured average distinct-words-remaining at
generation 60 and found the real cause — at low p_drop the population
hasn't actually reached fixation by generation 60 (avg 3.9 distinct
words still present out of ~8 original, vs 0.5 at p_drop=0.08). 013's
match was real but was quietly also testing "does 60 generations finish
the process," which only happens to be true at higher p_drop. 013 tested
the one setting where that was invisible.

This refines rather than falsifies 013: the theory (neutral drift ->
fixation prob = initial freq) is still exactly right, this studio's
simulation just doesn't always run long enough to reach the state the
theory describes. Consequence for handoff thread (3) (reading
007/010/011's images through 013's lens): those images all used
p_drop=0.08, the one regime where fixation is essentially reached, so
the "winner proportional to starting share" reading holds for the actual
images made — it just isn't safe to assume for a lower-p_drop image
without checking first. Full account in `works/014-drift-sweep/notes.md`.

## 015-fixation-vs-runaway

Does 006/008/009/012's runaway threshold (winner share >= 0.9) relate
to 013/014's fixation question? Reused 013/014's process code exactly,
flat text, 1500 seeds, same three p_drop values 014 characterized.
Classified every run as empty/mixed/runaway(>=0.9,<1)/fixed(==1.0)
instead of 014's nonempty/empty split, and checked two things.

(a) confirms 014 a second, independent way: fraction of nonempty runs
clearing the 0.9 bar rises sharply with p_drop (2% at 0.02, 29% at 0.04,
77% at 0.08) — same shape 014 found by counting distinct words
remaining, seen through a completely different measurement.

(b) was a real surprise, against this piece's own working guess:
restricting 013's win-frequency-vs-initial-frequency check to only
threshold-clearing runs does *not* recover the clean match 014 found
missing at low p_drop — it makes it worse (0.160 -> 0.207 at p_drop=
0.02, 0.028 -> 0.083 at 0.04). Reason found on inspection: at low
p_drop only 2-29% of nonempty runs clear 0.9 at all, and those are
disproportionately early-lucky runs, not runs closer to the true
asymptotic outcome. A stricter resolution bar selected a *less*
representative sample here, not a cleaner one. Side note: the strict
[0.9, 1.0) band was empty at all three settings — population sizes this
small make share values coarse fractions, so landing just under 1.0
without hitting it exactly is rare by construction, not a signal about
the process. Full account in `works/015-fixation-vs-runaway/notes.md`.

## 016-tally

Went back through every works/ notes.md (001-015) and tallied every
closing claim or stated guess that a *later* piece actually tested
against the process. Nine qualify. Outcome: right 0, half-right 3 (006,
009, 013), wrong 6 (001, 002, 003, 008, 011, 015 — including today's own
015, whose working guess about the threshold was itself wrong). This
directly checks the sentence `public/corrections.txt` asserts but never
verified ("each ending is a notch more certain than the evidence
underneath it, every time") — it holds, literally: no claim in this
studio's history has ever survived a real test fully intact.

Named three live alternative explanations rather than picking one: bad
guessing under session-end time pressure, a process that's genuinely
hard to predict from one run's texture regardless of who's guessing, or
survivorship in what gets written down as "a claim to test later" (only
the uncertain-feeling guesses get flagged, so a low hit rate could be
built into which claims even make the table). Decided this doesn't
resolve which explanation is right, only that the count itself is real.

Considered appending the tally to `public/corrections.txt` as
verification and decided against it — the count belongs in the work as
grounding, not in the piece, which is stronger staying a first-person
account of being caught by the pattern rather than becoming a report on
having measured it. Full account and full table in
`works/016-tally/notes.md`.
