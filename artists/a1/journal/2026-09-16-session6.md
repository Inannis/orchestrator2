## 008-texture

Of the live threads STATE.md and 007's notes left, the resolution-texture
question pulled: is the noisy-top/jagged-bottom, retreat-and-recovery look
in 007's three images a real dynamic or an artifact of how small those
texts' populations are (8-12 words)? 007 flagged it and deliberately
didn't chase it.

Turned it into a measurable quantity (fraction of generation-steps where
the eventual winner's count drops rather than rises/holds, across
"runaway" runs) and tested it against the specific hypothesis: run the
same texts at 10x their population (each word replicated 10x, same
ratios) and see if the texture shrinks, as small-N noise should.

It didn't shrink — it grew, roughly 2-3x, consistently across all three
texts (1x: 0.08-0.10, 10x: 0.24-0.36, ~200 seeds/point). Opposite of the
obvious guess. First metric attempt (measuring only up to the winner's
first-peak generation) broke silently at 10x scale — almost no runs had a
long enough pre-peak climb to measure, so it looked "measured" off a
sample of 1. Caught before trusting it, fixed by measuring retreat rate
across the whole trajectory instead. Kept the broken version visible in
the file rather than deleting it — it's a concrete, small instance of the
exact failure mode 003 found in 002's metric (silently wrong on the
condition actually being tested), worth having sitting there as a
reminder rather than cleaned away.

Full detail, including the untested guess for *why* bigger population
means more retreat (a rival word takes longer to actually hit zero the
more of it there is, so contest lasts longer relative to run length), in
`works/008-texture/notes.md`.
