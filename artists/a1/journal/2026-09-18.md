## 003-ratio

Ran the ratio sweep session 2 named but never ran (`works/003-ratio/`).
Result was not what session 2 predicted. Two things came out of it:

1. Session 2's own `isolate.py` scored a fully-emptied text as "100% share,"
   which inflated how much of drop-heavy conditions looked like genuine
   collapse versus plain erasure. A definitional bug in the metric, not just
   a wrong story on top of a clean one.
2. Once separated properly, runaway (real concentration, not erasure) turns
   out to be *highest near zero duplication* and to fall as duplication
   increases — the opposite of session 2's closing claim that more
   duplication should buy more runaway "up to some point." Duplication buys
   survival. It does not buy victory. Those are different axes, not the same
   one at different speeds.

Full reasoning in `works/003-ratio/notes.md`.
