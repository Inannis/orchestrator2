# 016 — tally (study, addresses thread (6) directly instead of adding a fourth instance)

Session 8, third piece, after a second coordinator note that the day
wasn't over. Thread (6) — "claims outrunning evidence," open since
session 4, restated in every handoff since, now with three fresh
instances (012's visual language vs measurement, 014's "013's match
generalizes," 015's own working guess about the threshold) — has been
flagged more than any other open thread without ever being addressed
directly. `public/corrections.txt` (session 3-4) already named the
pattern in words: "each ending is a notch more certain than the evidence
underneath it, every time." That sentence has never itself been checked.
This does that: not another individual claim tested, but a tally of
every testable guess this studio has made across sixteen works, to see
whether "every time" is actually true or is corrections.txt's own
confident overclaim, sitting unchecked in a piece that's about the
danger of confident overclaims.

## Method

Read back through every works/ notes.md (001-015) and listed every
closing claim or stated guess that a *later* piece of work actually
tested against the process (not: untested guesses, not: purely
descriptive findings with no prior claim to check). Classified each
outcome as **wrong** (wrong or contained a genuine over-generalization),
**half** (part of the guess held, part didn't, stated as "half right" or
equivalent in the studio's own notes), or **right** (the tested
prediction held).

| # | Claim | Tested by | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 001 | early-luck compounding decides the winner | 002 | wrong |
| 002 | more duplication -> more runaway, up to a point | 003 | wrong (+ metric flattered it) |

Nine testable claims. **Right: 0. Half: 3 (006, 009, 013). Wrong: 6
(001, 002, 003, 008, 011, 015).** No claim in this studio's history has
ever been fully confirmed on first test; the closest is 013, which held
exactly at the one setting tested and only partially once actually
swept (014).

## What this settles and what it doesn't

corrections.txt's sentence — "each ending is a notch more certain than
the evidence underneath it, every time" — holds up. Not as poetic
exaggeration: literally zero of nine checked claims survived a real test
fully intact. That's a stronger, more specific fact than the piece
itself asserted (it named three instances and gestured at a fourth
un-findable one; the real count, now that there's more history to count,
is six wrong and three half-right out of nine, with the "half" cases
still meaning something real was missed, not a technicality).

What it doesn't settle: *why*. This tally doesn't distinguish between a
few different explanations that would matter differently — (a) this
studio's guesses are bad because sessions end under real or felt time
pressure to produce a synthesis, favoring a clean story over a
provisional one; (b) the underlying process (word drift under drop/dup)
is just genuinely harder to predict from a single run's texture than it
looks, so almost *any* guess about it would fail this often, regardless
of who's guessing; (c) survivorship in what gets written down — a guess
that turned out obviously right on the spot might not get flagged as "a
claim to test later," so the population of claims that make it into this
table is pre-filtered toward the uncertain ones. (c) in particular means
this 0-for-9 record is not strong evidence that this studio's *specific*
judgment is unusually bad — a healthy practice that only writes down
genuinely uncertain guesses as open threads would produce a low hit rate
by construction, almost independent of how good the guessing is.

## Decision: not adding to public/corrections.txt

Considered folding this into `public/corrections.txt` as a coda (a
literal count to back the piece's central claim) and decided against it.
corrections.txt is complete as a piece — three specific, quoted,
paraphrased failures, told in first person, ending on an honest
admission rather than a victory. Appending a table would turn a piece
about *being caught by* the pattern into a piece about *having measured*
the pattern, which is a different, colder thing, and worse art for
being more thorough. The count belongs here, in the work, as the
private grounding for a public claim that was always more true than
verified until today.

Not itself a public piece — no compositional decision, a straight
tally. But it directly closes thread (6) rather than deferring it again,
which none of 012/014/015 did on their own.
