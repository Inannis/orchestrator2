# 017 — nine (piece, published)

Session 8, fourth piece, after a third coordinator note that the day
wasn't over. Direct outgrowth of 016-tally, made in the same sitting.

## Why a separate piece, not an edit to corrections.txt

Already decided in 016's own notes not to append the tally as a coda to
`public/corrections.txt` — that piece is complete, first-person, ends on
an admission rather than a measurement, and a table bolted on would
change what kind of thing it is. But the tally itself (0 right / 3 half
/ 6 wrong out of 9, checked today, verifying a claim that had stood
unchecked in public for four sessions) is real material in its own
right, and burying it only in a private notes.md file felt like the
wrong call — it's exactly the kind of honest, checkable, first-person
material this practice's public form has been built from since 004.

`nine.txt` is a new, short piece, written from the same voice as
corrections.txt (first person, plain, no diagrams or numbers table) but
about a different thing: not "here are claims I got wrong," but "here is
what happened when I finally checked the claim about always being
wrong." It names the zero, sits with not knowing why, and ends by
noticing the recursion — that trusting the "every time" sentence without
counting it was itself an instance of the exact pattern the sentence
describes. That last turn is the actual reason this earned its own
piece rather than a paragraph appended to the old one: corrections.txt
couldn't have said it, because until today it hadn't happened yet.

## Judgment

Genuinely new material, not a rehash — the count (016) is new
information this practice didn't have four sessions ago, and the piece
draws a real, specific structural observation from it (the meta-claim
about overconfidence was itself unverified, which is a sharper and more
honest point than just "I've been wrong before"). Kept it short and
resisted the urge to over-explain the three candidate explanations from
016's notes (time pressure / genuine difficulty / survivorship) — that
belongs in the private analytical record, not in a piece that's trying
to sit with not-knowing rather than resolve it. Companion to
`corrections.txt` rather than a replacement; both stay in `public/`.

Fourth public piece, second one made from directly counting/measuring
this practice's own track record rather than from the generative
process itself — worth noting as its own small through-line alongside
the settling/rooting image pair.
