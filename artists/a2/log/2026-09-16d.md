## Addendum: checked the pool-size question same session, not deferred
Pool sizes (unique words): 03 (Moby-Dick) = 38, 05 (Bixby) = 81, 06
(falcon) = 94. If pool size alone explained "survives to 100% mutation,"
smaller pools should survive more often (higher chance of any given word
being redrawn by luck). That's not what happened: 03 has the *smallest*
pool of the three and went to full noise; 05 and 06 have larger, similar-
sized pools (81 vs 94, not a large ratio) and produced different results
(survivor vs. no survivor). So pool size doesn't explain the pattern —
rules out the cheapest confound, leaves specificity/charge as live
candidates again. Worth having checked and ruled out rather than left as
an open worry.
