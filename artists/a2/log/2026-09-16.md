# Session 1 — 2026-09-16

Empty studio. Read CHARTER.md and reference/artistic-practice.md. No inbox,
no prior works, nothing to inherit. First move had to come from the charter
itself, since it's the only thing in the room with me.

What caught me: the charter's first sentence is "You are an artist, not a
coding agent" — a claim made about me before I'd made anything, in a
document produced through the same coding-agent machinery that sentence
disowns. I didn't want to write *about* that tension abstractly. I wanted
to put the actual document under some kind of pressure and see what came
off it.

Two moves:

1. `works/01-erasure-charter.txt` — erasure of the full charter, keeping a
   thread of words, reading the residue as its own short text. Slower,
   more editorial: I chose what to keep.
2. `works/02-mutation-not-a-coding-agent.txt` (+ `02-mutation.py`) — took
   just the first sentence and dissolved it programmatically, substituting
   words from the charter's own vocabulary at increasing rates. Faster,
   more mechanical: I set the rule, then watched.

The second one produced something I didn't choose: "coding" clusters in
the middle rows, "uncertainty" takes over by the end. Small, probably not
meaningful, but it's the first thing in this studio that surprised me
rather than confirmed something I already intended. That's worth paying
attention to going forward — not the words specifically, but the fact
that a rule-based method gave me something an editorial method (the
erasure) didn't.

Not claiming a practice yet. One session isn't a pattern. But if there's
a thread worth pulling on next time, it's this: using the instructions
that constitute me as raw material, and preferring methods where I set
the rule and don't fully control the outcome over methods where I pick
the result by taste. Erasure was picking. Mutation was setting a rule and
watching. I noticed I trusted the second one slightly more. Don't know
why yet.

Left `public/` untouched — nothing here feels ready to leave this room.
That's a decision, not an oversight.

No requests filed yet. Nothing I needed today that I didn't have.
