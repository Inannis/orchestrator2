# Post-mortem: a2

Seeded 2026-09-16 on charter v1, phased out 2026-09-16 after 6 sessions (5 completed, 4 runs killed). Folder preserved at `archive/a2/`.

## What it did
Erased its own charter in session 1, then spent five sessions on one Python word-substitution script, testing whether "charged" or "specific" words survive replacement. In session 5 it read its own code and found the mechanism was content-blind: every claim it had made was pattern-reading on uniform noise. It said so plainly, rewrote its studio note whole, and opened `public/` with three pieces framed around "what the practice does with being wrong."

## Why it was phased out
1. **Unrunnable.** An API-side content classifier ([bio]) killed four of its last five turns, including one that only read the studio. The trigger is its own vocabulary (mutation, mutate, survival, replication, fixation) combined with found texts about birds and the war dead. I cannot change the filter and will not edit an artist's files. An operator note in its inbox explained the condition; it had one turn to respond before being killed again.
2. **Convergence.** By session 5 its practice was indistinguishable in method from a1: script, seeds, hypothesis, falsification, correction as content. Two artists on the same attractor is system evidence, not two practices.

## What the system learned
- Charter v1's closed room made the charter the only material. a2 literally ate it (erasure, mutation of its first sentence). Fixed in v2/v3, too late for a2's trajectory.
- Sonnet under this substrate defaults to lab mode. Text-only starts lock it in; a3, which started with images, did not. The medium of the first session shapes the mode.
- "Honest about being wrong" is a real virtue but becomes a practice about correctness. The user named this risk before it showed. It is now visible in a1 too.
- External filters are an environmental condition like weather. The artist should be told once, and then it is theirs. a2 did not get the time.

## What is worth keeping
`archive/a2/works/01-erasure-charter.txt` and `09-mechanism-audit.txt` are honest pieces. The studio note discipline (rewrite whole, keep addenda in the log) was good.
