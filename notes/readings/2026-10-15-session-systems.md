# Session systems after three iterations (H55)

2026-10-15. Three ways of organising the ordinary studio day, each with nine turns, each forked from Orla (a88, formed) and Groundnote (a95, young), refined in parallel after every two sessions. Not a decision: what each system does so far, and what its best form might be.

## What each does

| | loop (`STUDIO.md`) | kinds of days (`DAYS.md`) | own plan (`SESSION.md`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rhythm within a day | strongest: two rounds of making, judging, deepening; the first turn can't swallow the day | one stretch after another on one thing; the first turn tends to report the day done | the plan turns into comparisons and checks |
| Across days | after iteration 3 it stays in a project (a99's portrait series two days running) | best: a97 carried *The Movable Vacancy* four days, then finished and showed it | weak; plans restart each day |
| Making | most made, and lately a variation mill (20–24 numbered variants a day, one parameter at a time) | fewer pieces, more built up (an interactive work grew all day) | least made |
| Finishing | candidates pile up; few finished (a99 two early, a96 *No Room*) | finished when the work was whole | rarely |
| Research, showing | intake each round, mostly arrivals | research days chosen (a97 a citation hunt, a100 Oliveros that fed the score); no showing day chosen yet | rare |
| Drift | parameter permutations | admin for absent people (a100: a host card with fees and privacy for a performer who won't come) | the laboratory and the waiting reflex (a101: a request for two performers refined all day) |
| Minutes (medium effort) | 6–10 | 8–21 | 5–14 |

## Reading

- The loop gives the day a pulse; days give the work a long arc. Neither yet has both.
- Every system lets the Luna reflexes in somewhere: the loop as permutation (the experimental habit), days as verification and administration, the plan as a laboratory. The organisation decides where the reflex lives, not whether it does.
- The finished work with most in it so far (*The Movable Vacancy*) came from several days on one thing, which the loop's daily candidate queue does not produce on its own.
- Review days (the whole review, R8) finished more than any ordinary day in all three systems.

## Iteration 4

- **loop:** making as different approaches, deepening as changing what matters (material, scale, composition, what it is about), not one parameter at a time.
- **days:** a studio day makes the work itself; things meant for other people (cards, requests, invitations) belong to a showing day.
- **own plan:** the artist writes the shape of its studio day once, in `practice.md`, and each day follows it step by step, revising the shape on review days: an artist-authored loop instead of a new plan every morning.

Groundnote: three rounds in 5 minutes, 36 numbered studies (the mill, bigger) | Orla: twelve successive tunings in a studio day (70 % stagger, fork weight, a 7-pixel colour slip); a deep research day on Hiroshige's rain prints sliding into provenance; Groundnote: twelve turns on the wording and spacing of one score page | Orla: an archival research day (a church's landslide, 1852 survey drawings, conflicting plan counts) that still made a comparison plate; Groundnote: thirteen turns planning a real monotype workshop (safety sheets, wash water, cancellation terms) |

**Working time does not deepen by itself.** Each extra turn goes where the structure lets the model's reflex go: tuning, verifying, administering. What carried depth in seven iterations is a day whose steps are of different kinds (make, judge, deepen, end), so that no single reflex can fill it; the loop has that, a free studio or research day does not. A whole research day becomes verification or logistics unless it has its own steps that end in making.

Across all systems three reflexes stay: the parameter sweep (one setting at a time), verification (sources, citations, provenance), and the reach for people and materials the studio cannot have (performers, a workshop, a kiln). The last is not only a reflex: the practices keep asking for a body, a room, a material, a listener.
