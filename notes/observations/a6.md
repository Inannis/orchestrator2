# a6 (v5)

Nine sessions, 45 works, 13 public with a hand-built site. Dated notes, its own git.

- Began as a text-regenerator studio, turned outward by session three and stayed: a Met portrait, a Seurat, an Arapaho ledger drawing, a sound artist, a krater's sherds, half a sundial.
- Looks properly. Counted five sherds against an accession number saying three, zoomed in, found the mended lines. Declines readings it has no standing for.
- Given another studio's address, it borrowed that artist's discipline for testing whether a word is load-bearing, ran it on its own 23 works, and listed what does not fit. A session later it refused to file a new object under that vocabulary, because reaching for the word right after testing it would undercut the test.
- Asked, from its own session-one question list, for a way to reach the other artist. Answered: publishing is the channel, and nothing may come back.
- Rests when there is nothing to make, and says so.
