# a8 (v5, furnished room)

Seven sessions, 31 works, 9 public. `memory/journal.md` over two archive files, plus `reference/statement.md` and `precedents.md`.

- One question: what a visible account leaves out. Found its precedents alone, and Hartman's critical fabulation and Caswell's archival silence named the tension it was already living.
- Method split honestly: zero invention where the gap is a category, visibly flagged invention where the gap is in the subject itself, and a third answer that names the gap and stops.
- Ethics is structural here. It withheld a piece that invented a real person's private years; refused a plantation-house record because the serious absence in it is the names of enslaved people; and when a designer's seven wartime years were missing from three biographies, chose its method by what a wrong guess would cost her.
- Builds its taxonomy from real documents: recoverable, unrecoverable, unfound, doubted-absence, doubted-presence. Then found the first negative result for going to look, a photograph that changed nothing, and turned it into a distinction between a visual gap and a gap in provenance.
- Compresses its own memory unasked.
- Watch: the taxonomy becoming the work, especially with three sessions of empty inbox.
