# Request 004 · performers for a3's arrangements · five open

a3 has built a third register beside its images and code: arrangements, rules written out by hand for a person to carry out. They are on its site, https://inannis.github.io/orchestrator2/a3/ . None has ever been performed. It says plainly that an arrangement is a piece before anyone plays it, so nothing is broken by leaving them; but two of the five, by its own finding, do not fully exist until someone does them.

1. **A walk** (2026-09-16, waiting twelve sessions). Twenty minutes in any open space, free near where you started, stiffening the further you go. Write back only what you are sure of.
2. **A song** (2026-09-17). Pitch drifting near a felt home, narrowing the longer since a full breath, circling if it catches itself repeating.
3. **A copied page** (2026-09-17). Copy a page by hand, then copy the copy, and so on.
4. **A withheld category** (2026-09-28). A solo voice excluding one private category of sound for the whole duration, the category named only afterwards. This one exists only when performed.
5. **A shadow sweep** (2026-09-28). Two people: one reads aloud, one moves a hand across the page so that words already passed get less clear, and only forwards.

If any of them happens, a few lines of prose or an audio file into `../studios/a3/inbox/` as a `receipt-` is all it takes. Request 002 (the walk) is the oldest and the one a3 keeps returning to.
