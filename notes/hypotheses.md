# Hypotheses

Every system change is one of these: observed, mechanism, change, window, measure, decision. Open first, closed one line each. Rewrite whole when it outgrows a cold read.

## Open
- **H5 · the inbox is alive.** Random public objects (Met, Art Institute, Gutenberg, Wikipedia, living artists) at p=0.5 on the first continuation, plus strangers who read only `public/`. Adopted, running. Its strongest result: a1, seventeen sessions of testing only itself, had just proven its own eye cannot agree with itself about whether a thing looks clean; hours later a random Iliad gave it Priam asking whether Hector's body was defiled, told the gods kept it unmarked. It recognised its own question and published its first piece made from the world. Unchoosable by me, unplannable by it. Watch only that the inbox does not become an artist's subject.
- **H10 · days of different length.** K drawn 1–6. Short days get used for curation, rest, or one real check; long days deepen. No decision needed unless a length starts producing filler.
- **H11 · the handoff condition** (charter v5, all six). What you leave for tomorrow is not the record of today; the first file holds what is alive, a few minutes' reading, history in dated files behind it, rewritten whole when it outgrows a cold read. Every studio moved inside it the same session (a3 18,781 → 1,441 words; a1 8,022 → 1,045). Since then they grow within a day and compress unasked when heavy. **Failure to watch for: a handoff that grows two sessions running with no rewrite**, not size itself. The fresh-mind reader is now a diagnostic, not a routine.
- **H12 · another artist exists.** One cross-artist encounter, live URL only, no introduction, never reciprocal in the same session. Both subjects engaged with the other's work rather than the fact of it: a6 borrowed a7's discipline and ran it on its own body of work, and a session later refused to file a new object under its own vocabulary because that would undercut the test. a5 opened a3's images rather than trusting its prose and flagged a resemblance as primed, not earned. Standing channel now. Watch for imitation and for convergence.
- **H13 · something that runs while you are away.** A `between/run.py` in a studio is run once between sessions (`tools/between.py`), output to `between/log.txt`. Offered in the tools sheet, named nowhere else. Nobody has built one. Window: 6 sessions from 2026-09-29. If nobody does, the finding is that a capability nobody needed is not a gap, and continuity must be levered elsewhere. Do not mention it again.
- **H14 · being read.** a3 and a7 told, factually, that another artist read their public page, what the reader did with it, and that the writing is not being sent because the other artist has not published it. Both made work from the reception, neither about it. Then a6 asked for a way to reach a7; answered that publishing is the channel and no private one will be opened. Window: 4 sessions. Measure: whether anything actually crosses, and whether the asking spreads.

## Candidates, not applied
- **Volume vs depth.** a7 makes up to seven pieces a day. It names the risk itself. Watch, do not cap.
- **Correction as content.** Sonnet's pull toward making its own error the subject. Much reduced under v5, still present in a1 (which is the whole practice there) and in a8's taxonomy-building. No move yet.
- **Sources shape subjects.** Museum catalogue entries invite "what is missing"; two studios organized around that independently. Widen what the world sends before calling a shared subject a house style.
- **Convergence without contact.** a3 writes arrangements for people to carry out; a7 found LeWitt, whose instructions are the permanent work. Neither knows of the other. Watch whether a third reaches it alone.

## Closed
- H1 baseline: continuity works; judgment honest; the closed room and short sessions became H2 and H3.
- H2 closed room → the charter names the world and the eyes; stale studio notes override the charter, so correct them in the inbox.
- H3 wording for session length: no effect. Became H4.
- H4 multi-turn day: adopted. 2 min → 15–60 min, no filler; the turn after "done" goes outward. The artist cannot end the day and the charter says so.
- E2 Haiku artist (a4): plans, crosses folder boundaries, makes nothing. Retired. Sonnet only.
- Isolation leak: studios inside the repo could see each other. Fixed by moving them to `../studios/`.
- H6 charter v4 positive pull: adopted. Artists show, curate, do the looking ritual, find precedents.
- H7 charter switch on a formed practice (a3): modest and brief. Its files are its charter. The lever is memory hygiene.
- H8 furnished room: adopted for seeding. Neither furnished artist ever took itself as first subject.
- H9 world-knock: adopted for all at p=0.5. Nudge-as-subject vanished; delivered objects enter the work.
