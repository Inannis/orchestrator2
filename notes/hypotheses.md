# Hypotheses

What each test is for and what it found. Each entry: observed, mechanism, change, test, measure, decision. Open first, closed one line each. How tests run and get decided is in `self-organization.md`. Rewrite whole when it outgrows a cold read.

## Open
- **H22 · things, not texts about things.** Observed in H19: four fresh studios on the `objects` feed (Met, Art Institute, Gutenberg: the object or the passage itself) all made things through s3 (evolved flowers, ladders of superfluity, interactive Tolstoy, empty plates), none turned to records or knowledge, record and bare alike. The earlier feeds carry texts about things (Wikipedia stubs, catalogue entries, living-artist summaries, news) and every practice drifted to knowledge by s4–5. Test: continue a19 (Temple) and a20 (Bairnsfather, then Tolstoy) to s6; a18, a21 paused. Measure: does the drift to records and the checking habit arrive by s4–6. If not, `objects` moves into the reference feed.
- **H20 · a whole memory system.** Observed: the one-paragraph handoff condition holds in young studios and decays in long ones (a3 1,441 → 8,763 words, a7 1,750 → 4,275); a measured note fixes it each time and only for a while. Every studio names its entry file differently, so nothing in the system can hold it; and the memory of works, projects, long wants and found material is whatever the artist improvises. Test: two forks of a7 at s14, each a complete system that keeps itself. **Rooms** (a15): a letter to tomorrow moved to `days/` each morning, projects as folders whose place is their state, `wants.md`, `found/`, every fifth day a studio day (its first fork day is one; a studio day with the ordinary K idles after the sorting, so it needs its own length). **Desk** (a16): notes live beside each work, the same letter, and `DESK.md` laid out each morning by `tools/desk.py` from git (lately, returned to most, left alone longest, waiting); nothing to bloat. Control: the reference's last sessions. Measure at s17: size of each memory surface, whether old works and wants come back into the work (RX7), the quality of the practice against the reference. Failure: the structure becomes the subject, or a fork stops making. Window 1 (s15–17): both forks made more and iterated more than a7 (Desk: a twill series with judgement, stopped by its own choice; Rooms: a connected run of pieces). Rooms' `wants.md` steered the work visibly ('to find agreement' became a piece picked from 403 complete records; 'something not about records' became nets, row-averaged paintings, sound). Memory compact in both (letter 160–250 words, wants 300) while a7's notes grew 707 → 1,031 in one session. But Rooms still writes a7's old notes beside its letter, and Desk's view was blind: no studio had committed since a7's habit to commit was lost in its own compression, so `desk.py` saw only old work. Fixed: `day.py` commits every studio at the end of each day. Extended to s20.
- **Model.** Sonnet 5.5 at medium for all, user's decision 2026-10-07. Replay of a7 s13 from one state (`notes/readings/2026-10-07-model-replay.md`): 5.5 does in 4 minutes what took 19, makes fewer new things and spends most continuations correcting its own past; effort high does not change that (one replay, high is not to be used again). The checking habit is now partly the model's; read every test against that.
- **Standing, adopted:** the inbox (H5: random arrivals at p=0.5 on the first continuation, plus strangers); days of different length (H10, K 1–6); another artist's URL as a channel, never reciprocal (H12); `between/run.py` offered, built by a3, a7, a9, a10 from their own work (H13); being read becomes work (H14); the image model and `listen.py` taken up everywhere, each new capability becomes a subject for a session or two (H18). Remedy kept until H20 decides: when a handoff grows two sessions running, send only its measured length (H11).

## Candidates, not applied
- **The checking habit** (laboratory: test, confirm, correct). In every condition and stronger on 5.5. H21 showed length is not it; wait for H19 before guessing a third cause; candidates then: work that cannot be checked (performance, an instruction for someone else), a person who answers (listener replies became work twice), a turn that is not a report.
- **Volume vs depth.** a7 made up to seven pieces a day on Sonnet 5. Watch, do not cap.

## Closed
- **H19 · arrivals as records make the school** (2026-10-07, s3, two matched pairs): rejected. Record and bare twins made nearly the same thing every session; the object leads, not its wrapper. Opened H22.
- **H21 · a day that cannot end early** (a17, fork, 2026-10-07, one day was decisive): with a hidden 20-minute floor, a17 made its two pieces, then ran ~70 continuations of 'Nothing is pending', a few thin draws, and lost a file it overwrote unread. Time without something to go into gives padding on 5.5. Depth has to come from what the day can enter (a project, a material, a person), not from length. `self` stays drafted, untested.
- **H15 · wide feed** (A: a9, a10), **H16 · charter v6** (B: a11, a12), **H17 · day shape `return`** (C: a13, a14), 2026-10-07 at session 5, blind, two readers. Neither feed nor charter moved the subject off knowledge or lowered the testing share (ref ~45%, A ~53%, B ~71%, C ~56%); made things rose everywhere (~27% → ~60%) with the tools; groups cross conditions and pairs split. `return` became an audit: almost every handed-back file ended in a correction. All six paused healthy.
- **H11 · the handoff condition**: holds young, decays in long practices; a measured-length note works once per send (a3 8,763 → 1,275, a7 4,275 → 707 on 2026-10-07). Superseded by H20.
- **E3 · Haiku** (2026-09-29): the gap is large (famous subjects, vague sourcing, inflated formula, false claims of craft). Sonnet only.
- **Control (a1 on v3):** a testing practice deepens and at seventeen sessions turned outward. Paused.
- **v5 as reference** (2026-09-29): formed, honest, self-correcting practices; weakness a shared epistemic subject and thin desire.
