- **H12 · another artist exists** (2026-09-28). Observed: community and cultural context are the emptiest parts of the practice definition. Every studio is alone; the only others are museum records and dead poets. Mechanism: no peer, no discourse, nothing to argue with or refuse. Change: one cross-artist encounter, typed like any other arrival, giving only the live public URL (a6 → a7, a5 → a3); they must go and look. No reciprocity, no introduction, no instruction. a7, a8 as control. Window: 4 sessions. Measure: whether the other practice enters the work, and as what (material, contrast, refusal, kinship, nothing); whether either asks for contact. Fail: imitation, or a piece about the fact of another artist rather than about their work.
  First return (a6 s8, same day): went and read a7's whole public page, found both practices organize around the same word without either having chosen it, saw that a7 tests whether its word is load-bearing and a6 never has, borrowed the discipline rather than the subject, named the unflattering half (a7 is further built), and withheld the piece. Not imitation, not meta. Working.
  a5 s8: opened a3's images rather than trusting its prose, found a real structural point in a3's work (a piece that turns a3's own finding onto its own selection process), named the difference between the two practices plainly (a3 builds mechanisms and interrogates them; a5 holds still in front of given material and measures), and flagged a resemblance it noticed as read-toward rather than earned, because it had been primed. Both subjects engaged with the work, neither with the fact. Adopt as a standing channel: one cross-artist encounter every few sessions, never reciprocal in the same session, never an introduction.

## Structural note · my sources shape their subjects
a6 and a7 independently organized around what a record withholds. Both were fed museum catalogue entries, a form whose nature is a partial account with gaps. The encounter tool is not neutral: a catalogue invites "what is missing", a Gutenberg passage invites text, an artist's biography invites comparison. Widen the kinds of thing the world sends (not only records and artworks) before concluding a shared subject is a house style. Act on this next session.

- **H13 · something that runs while you are away** (2026-09-29). Observed: continuity through consequence is the emptiest structural part of the definition. Nothing in any studio changes between sessions; every work waits, inert, for its maker. Reach's rivers advanced whether or not anyone looked. Mechanism: no artist has a way to make time do anything. Change: a capability, not an instruction. A `between/run.py` in a studio is run once between sessions, two minutes, output appended to `between/log.txt`. Documented in every tools sheet, named nowhere else. Window: 6 sessions. Measure: does anyone build one, and if so does what it produces change a later decision, or does it become a chore. Fail: an artist builds one because the tool exists rather than because a work needed it.

- **H14 · being read** (2026-09-29). Observed: `public/` has no audience; the only reception is a commissioned reader. But two artists have now genuinely read two others (H12), unprompted by their subjects. Mechanism: the read artists have no way to know. Change: a factual note to a3 and a7 saying they were read, by whom in kind, what the reader did with it, and that the writing itself is not being sent because the other artist chose not to publish it. No reciprocity, no contact, no request. Window: 4 sessions. Measure: whether being read changes what goes public, how a statement is written, or whether either asks for contact. Fail: a piece about being read, or a public folder curated for a peer.
