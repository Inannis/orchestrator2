# Hypotheses

What each test is for and what it found. Each entry: observed, mechanism, change, test, measure, decision. Open first, closed one line each. How tests run and get decided is in `self-organization.md`. Rewrite whole when it outgrows a cold read.

## Open
- **H23 · a layer that outlasts the works.** From H20: Rooms' `wants.md` steered the work visibly ('to find agreement' became a piece; 'something not about records' became nets, rows, sound), but Rooms ran a second memory beside its letter and narrowed after. Desk kept memory clean and gave the best series, but has no place for wants or interests that run under any one piece. Test: fork a16 at s20 as a23, `deskw` (desk plus `wants.md` and a studio day every fifth day). Window s21–25, to include one studio day. Measure: whether wants change what gets made, whether the desk and the wants stay small, whether studio days sort or idle.
- **Restart check for R2** (a22, fresh, from a Met drawing of a columbarium). Does R2 work from zero: by s5, does a22 make things with form, go deep on something of its own, and stay off the knowledge drift that every R1-era seed showed by s4–5? Compared with the R1 cohort and A/B/C at equal age, and with a16 as the accumulated R2. Afterwards keep the better pair as the reference.
- **Model.** Sonnet 5.5 at medium for all, user's decision 2026-10-07. Replay of a7 s13 from one state (`notes/readings/2026-10-07-model-replay.md`): 5.5 does in 4 minutes what took 19, makes fewer new things and spends most continuations correcting its own past; effort high does not change that (one replay, high is not to be used again). The checking habit is now partly the model's; read every test against that.
- **Standing, adopted:** the inbox (H5: random arrivals at p=0.5 on the first continuation, plus strangers); days of different length (H10, K 1–6); another artist's URL as a channel, never reciprocal (H12); `between/run.py` offered, built by a3, a7, a9, a10 from their own work (H13); being read becomes work (H14); the image model and `listen.py` taken up everywhere, each new capability becomes a subject for a session or two (H18). For R1 studios only: when a handoff grows two sessions running, send its measured length (H11); R2's desk leaves nothing to bloat.

## Candidates, not applied
- **The checking habit** (laboratory: test, confirm, correct). In every condition and stronger on 5.5. H21 showed length is not it; wait for H19 before guessing a third cause; candidates then: work that cannot be checked (performance, an instruction for someone else), a person who answers (listener replies became work twice), a turn that is not a report.
- **Volume vs depth.** a7 made up to seven pieces a day on Sonnet 5. Watch, do not cap.

## Closed
- **H20 · a whole memory system** (2026-10-07, forks a15 Rooms and a16 Desk, s15–20): Desk accepted into R2.
