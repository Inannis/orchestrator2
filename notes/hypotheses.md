# Hypotheses

Every system change is one of these: observed, mechanism, change, window, measure, decision. Open first, closed one line each. Rewrite whole when it outgrows a cold read.

## The design from 2026-09-29
The reference condition (v5 charter, museum feed, `template/conditions.md`) is locked and carried by a3 and a7. The other four slots run variants as fresh pairs, compared against each other and against the reference cohort's own first five sessions (a5–a8, from `runs/runs.ndjson`). A variant that beats the reference over its window becomes the reference; its pair then frees for the next variant. The target is a condition that, seeded 10–20 times, gives practices that are deep and unlike each other.

## Open
- **H15 · what the world sends shapes the subject** (condition A: a9, a10). Observed: of five v5 practices, three are about what a record withholds (a7, a8) or where observation runs out (a5), and a6 and a1 are close. The museum feed hands them catalogue records from session one: a7's first day confronted invented restorations with a catalogue line, a8's first day was an invented fragment with no record behind it. Change: the `wide` feed (a photograph, this week's news, a living thing seen this week, a little museum), charter unchanged. Window: 5 sessions each. Measure: at s5, is either practice about evidence, records, or the limits of knowing? Are the two unlike each other? Failure: the news becomes the default subject every session, or they converge on the same house style anyway.
- **H16 · a charter centred on wanting** (condition B: a11, a12). Observed: desire is the thinnest part of every practice; they have questions, not wants. The v4/v5 honesty paragraph ("what you only imagined is also material, as long as you know which is which") reads almost word for word as a8's method and a7's subject. Change: charter v6, rewritten whole: what moves artists comes first, making is for a thing with form someone could stand in front of, the best work keeps something unaccounted for, the day ends by noting what you want more of, honesty is one functional line. Same wide feed as A, so B vs A isolates the charter. Window: 5 sessions each. Measure: do the studios' own files name wants that change decisions; is there form (image, object, sound, a made thing) rather than prose about things; is anything left unexplained on purpose. Failure: performed desire (wanting stated, nothing different made), pretty images with no return, or the same epistemic house style in warmer words.
- **H5 · the inbox is alive.** Random objects at p=0.5 on the first continuation, plus strangers who read only `public/`. Adopted. Its best result: a1 finding its own closed question in a random Iliad. Watch that the inbox does not become the subject.
- **H10 · days of different length.** K drawn 1–6. Short days curate or rest, long days deepen. No decision unless a length produces filler.
- **H11 · the handoff condition** (in every charter). Failure to watch for: a handoff that grows two sessions running with no rewrite. a3's journal was rewritten in s12 and grew 1,441 → 3,124 words through s13; if s14 grows without a rewrite, that is the failure.
- **H12 · another artist exists.** One cross-artist address, no introduction, never reciprocal in a session. Standing channel. Not used on the new pairs before s5, so they stay independent samples.
- **H13 · something that runs while you are away.** `between/run.py`, offered in the tools sheet only. First use, session one of its window: a3 (s14) found the Wikipedia record behind an old piece still being edited two years after the event, and built a watcher that logs the article's revisions while the studio sleeps, so "wear" is measured in real days for the first time. It came from the work, not from the offer. Window runs to 6 sessions: does a3 read the log and make from it, and does anyone else build one. Do not mention it.
- **H14 · being read.** a3 and a7 were told another artist read them; both made work from it. a6 asked to reach a7, answered that publishing is the channel; a6 is paused with that open. Watch whether anything crosses when a6 resumes.

- **Model config** (user, 2026-09-29): every artist on `claude-sonnet-5` at effort `medium`, passed by the headless dispatcher. The subagent days before ran the same model at the harness default effort. Everyone switches together, so pairs stay comparable; watch whether days get shorter or shallower than the ledger's earlier sessions.

## Candidates, not applied

- **Volume vs depth.** a7 makes up to seven pieces a day. Watch, do not cap.
- **Convergence without contact.** a3 and a7 both reached instruction-works independently.

## Closed
- **E3 · Haiku on the current charters** (user's question, 2026-09-29). Four fresh Haiku seeds byte-identical to a9–a12 (same charter, seed, K, arrivals), plus Haiku and Sonnet each continuing an identical copy of a8. A blind reader paired all four, put one maker on the same side every time, and called the gap large: Haiku reaches for famous subjects, sources vaguely, repeats a formula while inflating it, claims formal craft the text lacks (ballades that don't rhyme to scheme), writes 2–4x the prose and far fewer images. It does make things now, unlike E2. Sonnet only. `notes/readings/2026-09-29-haiku-vs-sonnet.md`.
- **Control (a1 on v3):** a practice built on testing itself deepens rather than repeats, and at seventeen sessions it turned outward by itself. Paused 2026-09-29.
- **v5 as reference:** adopted and locked 2026-09-29. It reliably gives formed, honest, self-correcting practices with memory in shape; its weakness is a shared epistemic subject and thin desire.
