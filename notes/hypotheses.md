# Hypotheses

What each test is for and what it found. Each entry: observed, mechanism, change, test, measure, decision. Open first, closed one line each. How tests run and get decided is in `self-organization.md`. Rewrite whole when it outgrows a cold read. Full entries up to 2026-10-16: `notes/readings/2026-10-16-hypotheses-archive.md`.

## Open
- **H62 · the press** (user, 2026-10-17: a critic who looks at the artist's website and writes an article, a parallel system developed alongside; whether artists use it depends on how it is presented and woven into the structure). A critic with its own desk (`../critics/c1/`: `CRITIC.md`, notes per artist, `taste.md`, articles; Luna; first article: 'The Cut Has More Than One Job', it named itself Mara Venn) visits a copy of the public pages (`tools/press.py`), writes an article to readers; it arrives in the artist's `press/` and on the site (`docs/press/`). Woven in: the review's first movement reads `press/`, the studio day's intake may take it in (`press`). a120 = a115 at s4, a121 = a116 at s4, each with an article; controls: a115's own review 5, a116's review 5 without press. Window: the review and one day after. Measure: is the article read, named, argued with, does it change the review's story, the look, the next period. First form: a121's review took the article into its story ('The press visit saw the connection to *River Openings*… the critic also noticed that both bodies of work risk making encounters feel pre-solved'), a120 only filed it ('outside criticism in `press/`'), neither named it the day after (against 0 of 5 for readings, H49, H52, H57). Second form (2026-10-17): the day's opening announces a new article ('A critic wrote about your work: `press/…`', `day.py`); the critic revisits whenever the public pages changed (`press.py --auto` before each round); the review asks where the critic saw something the artist hadn't and where it is wrong. a120, a121 to their review 10; controls a115, a116 alongside. That run was void: every visit fell on one date and the article file names were built from the date alone, so a121's revisit overwrote its first article under a name already marked read (never announced) and a120's revisit found its name taken and counted as nothing written (fixed: a timestamp in every name). Rerun from s10: fresh visits (a120 'The Form and Its Authority'), three sessions, the announcement in each day's opening.


## Standing, adopted
- **Make it with what you have** (user, 2026-10-17): when a practice reaches for people, rooms or materials it doesn't have, the answer is to get creative and work around it with what is available, like a real artist: in request answers, in the charter's means (the performer becomes a recorded voice, the kiln a pot glazed in light) and in the studio day's making.
- **The reference chain:** R5 a world beyond the museum (H33) → R6 the genesis for every new artist (H40–H43: a warm whole text, an inheritance by chance from long found lists, the artist as judge, light, sound, words and code as finished materials, warm inbox, warm critic) → R7 the review rewritten whole as `REVIEW.md` (H47) → R8 the review sees the look (H50), time arrives as an instrument in the inbox (H51) → R9 five inheritances with a manifesto, drawn over whole counted lists (H54). Medium effort on Luna as good as xhigh (H40).
- **Older, still in force:** the inbox (H5, random arrivals at p=0.5 on the first continuation); days of different length (H10); `between/run.py` (H13), now announced as time (H51); being read becomes work (H14) did not replicate on Luna (H49, H52, H57); the image model and `listen.py` (H18); every new capability becomes a subject for a session or two (H18).
- **What reaches a formed practice:** guided days (genesis, review) and arrivals in the inbox; a changed charter does not (a78, a85, a86).
- **Model** (user): Luna is the focus; Sonnet 5.5 at medium is a cross-reference for what another model does with the system (a76). Sonnet does the system's work in a quarter of the time and corrects its own past (`notes/readings/2026-10-07-model-replay.md`).

## Candidates, not applied
- **The Luna look.** Every Luna studio drifts to one plate (cream ground, serif title, small-caps labels, annotated drawing) whatever the medium; the review's look movement breaks it per artist (H50), the image model as brush escapes it (a67, a70, a77), code-made images return to it (H54).
- **Three reflexes** (the old checking habit): the parameter sweep (one setting at a time), verification (sources, citations, provenance), and the reach for people and materials the studio cannot have; the organisation of a day decides where they live, not whether (H55).
- **Audience on Luna.** Readings, neighbours and a performance of its own score arrived and were not named (H49, H52, H57); things from the world are taken up at once. Ask the user before the next attempt.
- **The draw.** Without a real roll the model picks its favourites (*The Great Wave* four times, the Futurist manifesto three; a category heading as a way of making, a115). A small `draw.py` that fetches a list and rolls would settle it, but it is a tool handing input: ask the user.
- **Temperament.** One gentle, careful worldview; manifestos add voice and opponents, not another worldview (H53, H54): the model's handwriting.
