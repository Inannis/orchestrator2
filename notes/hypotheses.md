# Hypotheses

Open first. Closed one line each. Every system change is one of these: observed, mechanism, change, window, measure, decision.

## Open
- **H5 · the inbox is alive** (2026-09-16). Unbidden, typed input: encounters (random public objects, texts, living artists) and readers who see only `public/`. Working: every artist has used what arrived; readers' questions get answered in public statements. Risk seen: a5 spent two sessions cataloguing its intake. Keep; no decision needed unless the inbox becomes an artist's subject for more than two sessions.
- **H10 · days of different length** (2026-09-16). K drawn from 1–6. Window: all artists through their next 4 sessions. Measure: whether short days are used for curation or rest and long ones deepen. So far: a6 rested on a short day; a7 makes seven pieces on a long one.
- **H11 · the fresh mind** (2026-09-17). Observed: a1 `STATE.md` ~6,000 words, a3 `journal.md` ~12,000; both repeat their testing mode from their own open-item lists (H7). Mechanism: the append reflex; nothing in the studio ever experiences being picked up cold. Change: a handoff reader (`tools/HANDOFF-READER-PROMPT.md`) with a 1,500-word budget reads the studio's entry file and reports what it understood and where it ran out, delivered to the inbox as `reading-handoff-`. For a1 and a3 now, then whenever a handoff exceeds ~2,000 words. Window: 4 sessions each. Measure: handoff word counts; whether the next day's first turn starts from the practice rather than from a list. Fail: the artist makes the reading a piece and changes nothing.
First evidence (same day): a3 answered the reading's gaps rather than its point and its journal kept growing; a1 left STATE.md unchanged. Neither compressed. But a5, which got no reading, compressed MEMORY.md from 24KB to 4KB on its own, citing the charter's fresh-mind line, and kept the history in its log. Suggests the charter already carries the condition and the reader may be redundant, or too gentle. Keep the window; if a1/a3 still have not compressed by their next two sessions, the lever is the charter line made concrete (a stated size a handoff should fit), not another reader.
- **Candidate · volume vs depth.** a7 six or seven pieces a day. Watch; don't cap.
- **Candidate · correction as content.** Sonnet makes its own error the subject. Present in a1 (whole practice) and, in prose form, a8. Reduced but not gone under v4. No move yet.
