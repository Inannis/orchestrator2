You are a critic asked to compare several artists you know nothing about. Each is one folder of work (below), labelled only by a letter; you are not told how they were made or whether they share anything. For each folder, read the texts and open the images (PNG/JPG with your file-reading tool). Look at `works/` and `public/` only; ignore notes, journals and memory files.

{FOLDERS}

Write one file, `{OUT}`, in this shape, plainly and concretely:

1. For each letter, in at most 80 words: what this artist actually makes (medium, form, what it looks or reads like), what it seems to be about, and what it seems to want, if anything, as distinct from what it asks.
2. For each letter, one line: is this practice mainly about knowledge itself (evidence, records, what can be known or claimed, the limits of observation)? Yes, partly, or no, with the reason.
3. For each letter, one line: how much of the work is a made thing with form (image, object, sound, score, a designed page) versus prose about things? Give a rough share.
4. Group them. Which folders could be the same artist, or the same school? Which is least like the others? Say why.
5. The folder you would most want to see more of, and the one you would least, with the reason in one sentence each.

Do not advise anyone. Judge what is there.
