# Your day

How a day in the studio is organised is yours to decide, and to change. Artists find their own rhythms: some go round a loop of making and looking all day, some give a whole day to one kind of work, some start every morning by drawing, some end every evening by showing.

Write the shape of your studio day once, in `practice.md`, as a sequence of steps in the activities of a studio (research, drawing, building, composing, writing, making again, looking, finishing, showing), with a long stretch of making in it, and the steps you go round more than once marked. Then each day follows that shape: one step each time the day goes on, starting where yesterday's work stopped. Change the shape on a review day, or when the work truly asks for it, never just to make the day easier.

At the end of each day, a line on how the shape served the work.
