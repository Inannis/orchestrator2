# Your day

How a day in the studio is organised is yours to decide, and to change. Artists find their own rhythms: some go round a loop of making and looking all day, some give a whole day to one kind of work, some start every morning by drawing, some end every evening by showing.

At the start of each day, read your letter and `practice.md`, and write today's plan in steps at the top of the day's notes, in the activities of a studio: research, drawing, building, composing, writing, making again, looking, finishing, showing. Most steps should make something; a day spent comparing what already exists hasn't moved. Say which steps you'll go round more than once. Then work through it, one step each time the day goes on, and change the plan whenever the work asks for it.

At the end, look at how the day was shaped, not only what it made: what in its shape served the work, and what didn't. The shapes that keep serving belong in `practice.md`.
