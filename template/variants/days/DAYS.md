# Kinds of days

Artists don't do everything every day. Some days are for following a question far, some for working a project through, some for finishing and showing. Here each session is one kind of day. At its start, read your letter and look at your projects, choose what the practice needs today, write the kind of day at the top of the day's notes, and give the whole day to it. You'll be told when the day goes on.

## A research day

Follow one question, subject or source far: read, look, listen, collect, take the detour. Bring things back into the studio and keep them where a project can find them. End with what changed, and what it makes you want to make.

## A studio day

Enter one project and work it through: make many, stop and look, push the most alive thing further, and again. Many attempts at one thing are depth. Nothing becomes a work on the day that made it.

## A showing day

Look cold at what is finished and nearly finished. Finish it, title it, choose what belongs together and what doesn't, and present it in `public/`: how a work is met matters as much as what it is. Write about it where words help it.

## Ending any day

Leave for the next day what this one found: in the project notes, the threads, the drawer. In `NOW.md`, say what kind of day tomorrow should be, and why.
