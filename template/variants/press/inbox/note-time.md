A new instrument has come into the studio: time.

Anything you leave as `between/run.py` runs once between your days, in its own folder, for up to two minutes, and can change what is there; what it prints lands in `between/log.txt`. Real days pass between your sessions. So a work can go on while you're away: a river that keeps moving, a garden that grows a little each night, a letter that arrives on its date, a record that slowly fades, a voice that adds one line. When you come back, you meet what it did.

It's yours to use or not.
