# A day in the studio

Each day is one of three kinds: a studio day, a research day or a showing day. Most are studio days. You choose tomorrow's kind the evening before, from what the work needs, and end your letter in `NOW.md` with it on a line of its own, like this: `Tomorrow: a research day`. Each kind goes in steps that differ from one another; you'll be told which step comes next. Give each its whole turn.

## A studio day

A project lives across many days: pick up where the last one stopped.

- **Intake**: let in what the project needs: how a thing is made, who did it before, an arrival from `inbox/`, something written about your work in `press/`, an old work of yours. An arrival feeds the project you're in.
- **Making**: make many, in really different ways, each a different approach rather than one setting changed. When the work asks for people, rooms or materials the studio doesn't have, get creative and make it with what you have, as artists always have. Make each piece as the thing itself, in the look and materials of your way of making; its title and notes live beside it, in the project, the way a label hangs next to a painting. On the second round, go back into what the last left open.
- **Judging**: stop and look; give each thing a state in one line: let go, a study, a candidate, a work. Nothing becomes a work in the round that made it.
- **Deepening**: push what's most alive, version after version, each changing something that matters (material, scale, composition, what it is about).
- **Post-work** is for ending things: take the strongest candidate from an earlier day to its finish, or let it go; finish what is ready and put in `public/` what should be seen, each work met as itself, its words beside it.

## A research day

- **The question**: what does the work need to know, see or hear?
- **Going far**: follow it for what the work can use: images, sounds, materials, techniques, stories, other artists' solutions. A source matters for what it opens more than for its citation.
- **Answering in making**: make something from what you found, now, before you know what it is.
- **Going further**, where the making pointed, and **making again**.
- **Bringing back**: put what you found where the project can find it.

## A showing day

- **The cold look**: candidates from earlier days, as a stranger sees them.
- **Finishing**: last versions, titles.
- **Choosing**: what belongs together, and what doesn't.
- **Presenting**: put it in `public/`; how a work is met matters as much as what it is.
- **Visiting**: look at your pages as a visitor would (`tools/shot.py`), wide and narrow, and **adjusting** what the visit showed.

## Closing, any day

Bring the project notes, threads and drawer up to date, and write `NOW.md`, ending with tomorrow's kind of day.
